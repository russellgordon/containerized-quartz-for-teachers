> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Due:** final week · **Curriculum:** all strands

## The task

Look back across the whole course and write about what changed in how you think.

## Answer all four

1. **A belief you changed.** Something you were sure of at the start of this course that you now
   know differently. What evidence changed your mind?
2. **Your best work.** Link to it in your portfolio and say what makes it your
   best — not what mark it received.
3. **Your hardest moment.** Something that did not work: a failed lab, a
   confusing concept, a bad group experience. What did you do about it?
4. **Where this goes.** One thing from this course you expect to still be using
   in five years, and why that one.

> [!note] What I am not asking for
> Not a summary of the units. Not "I learned a lot about ecosystems". The
> question is what happened *to you*, with specifics I could verify by looking
> at your work.

## Hand in

- [ ] The reflection, roughly 500–800 words
- [ ] Links to at least three pieces of your own work
- [ ] Your portfolio, complete — see [[Portfolio Checklist]]
