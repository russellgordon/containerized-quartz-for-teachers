> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Due:** end of the biology unit · **Format:** 4–6 pages or equivalent ·
> **Curriculum:** [[B1.1]], [[B1.2]], [[B1.3]], [[A2.4]]

## The task

Choose **one** climate impact that affects a real community, and build a plan
for responding to it.

## What to include

1. **The impact.** What is happening, where, and to whom? Use at least three
   sources, one of them a primary data source such as a government or research
   dataset.
2. **The mechanism.** Explain the science. Connect it to
   [[Indicators of Climate Change]] and [[The Greenhouse Effect]] — I want the
   physical chain of cause and effect, not just "the climate is changing".
3. **Who is affected, and unevenly.** Include how the impact falls on
   communities in Canada, and address at least one First Nations, Métis, or
   Inuit community's experience, using sources from that community wherever
   possible.
4. **Existing responses.** What is already being tried? What is working?
5. **Your plan.** Three actions, each with: who does it, what it costs, what it
   would achieve, and how you would measure whether it worked.

> [!tip] The most common weakness
> Plans that stop at "raise awareness". Awareness of what, by whom, leading to
> which decision? Name the decision your plan is trying to change.

## Assessment

| Criterion | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Understanding the science | Names the impact | Describes it | Explains the mechanism accurately | Explains it and its interactions with other systems |
| Use of evidence | Few sources, unexamined | Sources listed | Sources evaluated for reliability | Sources compared where they disagree |
| Communities | Mentioned generally | Described | Specific, sourced, respectful | Draws on community voices directly |
| Plan | Vague actions | Actions with some detail | Costed, measurable actions | Actions with trade-offs assessed honestly |
