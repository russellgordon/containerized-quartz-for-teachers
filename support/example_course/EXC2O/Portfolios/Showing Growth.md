## Do this

1. Re-read your [[First Entry]].
2. Find an early piece of work and a recent one on a **similar** skill — two lab
   reports, two graphs, two explanations.
3. Put them side by side and name three specific differences.

## Not this

> "I have improved a lot and learned many things."

## This

> "In my first lab report the conclusion was one sentence and repeated the
> hypothesis. In the Ohm's law report I used the slope of the graph, gave a
> percent difference from the marked value, and explained why the line did not
> pass through the origin. I did not know a graph could *be* the evidence."
