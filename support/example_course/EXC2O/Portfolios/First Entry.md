Write your first entry this week. Roughly 300 words, answering:

1. **Where you are starting.** What do you already like or dislike about
   science, and what happened to make you feel that way?
2. **What you expect.** One thing you hope to understand by June.
3. **Your worry.** One thing you expect to find hard. Naming it now makes it
   easier to notice later when it stops being hard.

> [!note] Honesty is the only requirement
> "I have never liked science and I am taking it because I have to" is a
> perfectly good first entry. It is also the entry that is most interesting to
> re-read in June.
