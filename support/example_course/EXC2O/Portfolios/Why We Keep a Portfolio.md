A mark on a test tells you where you stood on one morning. A portfolio shows
what changed over five months — and only you can build that.

## What goes in

- Work you are proud of, and **why**
- Work that went badly, and what you did next
- Questions you still have
- Connections you noticed between units

## What does not

- Everything. A portfolio is chosen, not accumulated.
- Marks. This is about the work, not the grade on it.

> [!tip] Write entries when the work is fresh
> The details you will want at the end of the course — what confused you, what
> you tried first —
> are gone by next week. Ten minutes on the day beats an hour later.
