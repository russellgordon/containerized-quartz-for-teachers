## Purpose

Determine how much of the electrical energy supplied to a bulb becomes light,
and how much becomes heat.

> [!danger] Safety
> Bulbs get **hot enough to burn**. Let them cool before touching. Watch for
> water near electrical equipment.

## Method

Direct measurement of light output needs equipment we do not have, so we measure
the **waste heat** instead, by warming a known mass of water, and treat the rest
as light.

$$
Q = mc\Delta T
$$

where $c = 4.18\ \mathrm{J/(g \cdot ^\circ C)}$ for water.

## Procedure

1. Record the bulb's power rating.
2. Suspend the bulb in a container holding $200\ \mathrm{g}$ of water without
   touching the sides.
3. Record the starting temperature.
4. Switch on for exactly 5 minutes, stirring gently.
5. Record the final temperature.
6. Repeat with an LED of similar brightness.
