## Purpose

Measure current and potential difference in series and parallel circuits, and
state the rules for each from your own measurements.

> [!danger] Safety
> Have every circuit checked before switching on. Never connect an ammeter
> directly across the battery.

## Procedure

### Part A — series

1. Build: battery, switch, two identical lamps in one loop.
2. Measure current at three points: before lamp 1, between the lamps, after lamp 2.
3. Measure potential difference across each lamp, then across both together.

### Part B — parallel

1. Rebuild with the two lamps on separate branches.
2. Measure current in each branch and in the main line.
3. Measure potential difference across each lamp.

## Analysis

1. Write a rule for current in series, and one for parallel, using your numbers.
2. Do the same for potential difference.
3. Which arrangement made the lamps brighter? Explain using total resistance.
4. Unscrew one lamp in each circuit. What happens, and why does that explain how
   houses are wired? See [[Series and Parallel Circuits]].
