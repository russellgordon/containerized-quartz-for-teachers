## The question

When emissions must fall, who should be made to change: individuals,
corporations, or governments?

## Positions to consider

| Position | The argument | The objection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individuals | Demand drives supply; habits scale | Personal footprints are a small share of the total |
| Corporations | A small number of firms produce most emissions | They produce what people buy |
| Governments | Only they can set rules that bind everyone | Rules can fall hardest on those least able to absorb them |

## Ground rules

- Attack arguments, never people
- "I disagree because…" — always finish the sentence
- You may change your mind out loud. That is a success, not a loss

> [!question] The complication worth raising
> The idea of a personal "carbon footprint" was popularised by an advertising
> campaign for an oil company. Does knowing that change how you weigh the first
> position? Should the origin of an idea affect whether it is true?
