Every expectation page in this folder is written **in our own words**. The
official wording belongs to the Ontario Ministry of Education, and this is a
sample course built to demonstrate software — not an official curriculum
document.

> [!important] If the wording matters, use the official source
> For assessment, reporting, or anything you would hand to a parent, quote the
> official text from the Ministry's site:
> [The Ontario Curriculum, Science, Grade 9 (SNC1W)](https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/secondary-science/courses/snc1w/strands)

Each page keeps the official **code** (`B2.3`, `D2.4`, and so on) so you can look
the real wording up in seconds. The codes are also tags, which means every page
tagged `#B2` can be gathered in one place.

## Why paraphrase at all?

Two reasons, and both are worth explaining to students:

1. **Curriculum documents are written for teachers.** "Demonstrate an
   understanding of the dynamic and interconnected nature of ecosystems" is
   precise, but it is not how you would explain the point of a unit to a
   fourteen-year-old.
2. **Rewriting something is how you find out whether you understood it.** That
   is as true for a teacher reading curriculum as it is for a student writing a
   lab conclusion.
