A single square holds four pieces of information:

| Position | What it is | Example (sodium) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top | Atomic number = protons | 11 |
| Middle | Symbol | Na |
| Below | Name | Sodium |
| Bottom | Atomic mass | 22.99 |

## Reading it well

- **Atomic number** is the identity of the element. Change it and you have a
  different element.
- **Atomic mass** is a weighted average across isotopes, which is why it is
  rarely a whole number. Round it to find the most common mass number.
- **Neutrons** = mass number − atomic number.
- **Group** (column) gives valence electrons for the main groups; **period**
  (row) gives the number of shells.

## Finding what you need quickly

1. Metal or non-metal? Left of the staircase is metal.
2. Which group? That gives the likely ion charge: group 1 → $1+$,
   group 2 → $2+$, group 16 → $2-$, group 17 → $1-$.
3. Full outer shell? Group 18 — expect no reaction.
