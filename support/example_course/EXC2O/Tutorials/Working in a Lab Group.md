Lab groups fail for social reasons far more often than scientific ones.

## Rotate the roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Manager | Watches the time, keeps the procedure on track |
| Technician | Handles equipment and materials |
| Recorder | Writes down every reading as it happens |
| Safety officer | Goggles, spills, and the question "what could go wrong here?" |

Rotate every investigation. The person who never touches the equipment does not
learn to use it.

## Rules that prevent most problems

- Everyone records their own data, even though one person calls it out.
- Disagree about a reading? Take it again. Do not average an argument.
- If you did not see it happen, do not write it down.

> [!note] Sharing data is not copying
> Groups may share **measurements** — you all measured the same thing. Analysis,
> conclusions, and answers to questions must be your own words and your own
> thinking. If two analyses read identically, both are a problem.
