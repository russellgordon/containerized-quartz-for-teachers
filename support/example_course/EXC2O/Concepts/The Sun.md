## The idea

The Sun is an ordinary star, and essentially every process on Earth's surface
runs on energy it sent us.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Diameter | $1.39 \times 10^{6}\ \mathrm{km}$ (about 109 Earths across) |
| Mass | $99.86\%$ of the entire solar system |
| Surface temperature | About $5\,500\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$ |
| Core temperature | About $15$ million $^\circ\mathrm{C}$ |
| Distance from Earth | $1.50 \times 10^{8}\ \mathrm{km}$ = 1 AU |
| Light travel time to Earth | About 8 minutes 20 seconds |

## Fusion is the power source

In the core, hydrogen nuclei fuse into helium. The helium weighs slightly less
than the hydrogen that made it, and that missing mass becomes energy:

$$
E = mc^2
$$

Because $c^2$ is enormous, a tiny mass difference releases a staggering amount
of energy — about $4$ million tonnes of mass per second.

> [!question] The strangest fact on this page
> A photon takes minutes to cross from the Sun to Earth, but can take a hundred
> thousand years to escape the Sun's interior — it is absorbed and re-emitted
> constantly. Sunlight on your face left the core before the last ice age.
