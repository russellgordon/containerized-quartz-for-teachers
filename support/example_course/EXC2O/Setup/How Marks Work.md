| Category | Weight | What it includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge and understanding | 25% | Tests, quizzes, concept checks |
| Thinking and investigation | 25% | Labs, design work, analysis |
| Communication | 20% | Reports, diagrams, presentations |
| Application | 30% | Tasks connecting science to real situations |

The final mark is **70% term work, 30% final assessment**.

## What I am actually assessing

Not how much you can recall. Whether you can **use** it: interpret unfamiliar
data, design a fair test, explain your reasoning to someone else.

> [!question] Why do labs count under Thinking rather than Knowledge?
> Because following a procedure is not the skill. Deciding what to measure,
> noticing when a result is suspicious, and judging what your data can support
> — that is thinking, and that is what earns the marks.

## Late work

Talk to me **before** the due date and we will make a plan. After the fact is
much harder for both of us.
