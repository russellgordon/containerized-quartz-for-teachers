Show your work: formula, substitution, answer with units.

1. $V = 12\ \mathrm{V}$, $R = 4.0\ \Omega$. Find $I$.
2. $I = 0.50\ \mathrm{A}$, $R = 24\ \Omega$. Find $V$.
3. $V = 9.0\ \mathrm{V}$, $I = 0.30\ \mathrm{A}$. Find $R$.
4. A kettle draws $12.5\ \mathrm{A}$ at $120\ \mathrm{V}$. Find its
   resistance.
5. A $60\ \Omega$ resistor carries $0.20\ \mathrm{A}$. Find the potential
   difference across it.
6. Two $10\ \Omega$ resistors in series across $12\ \mathrm{V}$. Find the
   total resistance and the current.
7. The same two resistors in parallel across $12\ \mathrm{V}$. Find the total
   resistance and the total current.
8. A phone charger supplies $5.0\ \mathrm{V}$ at $2.0\ \mathrm{A}$. Find its
   power and the energy delivered in 30 minutes.

## Answers

1. $3.0\ \mathrm{A}$ 2. $12\ \mathrm{V}$ 3. $30\ \Omega$
4. $9.6\ \Omega$ 5. $12\ \mathrm{V}$
6. $20\ \Omega$, $0.60\ \mathrm{A}$
7. $5.0\ \Omega$, $2.4\ \mathrm{A}$
8. $10\ \mathrm{W}$, $18\,000\ \mathrm{J}$
