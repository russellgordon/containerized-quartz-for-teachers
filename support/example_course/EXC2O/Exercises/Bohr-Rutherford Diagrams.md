For each atom: state protons, neutrons, and electrons, then draw the diagram.

| Element | Atomic number | Mass number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Helium | 2 | 4 |
| Carbon | 6 | 12 |
| Oxygen | 8 | 16 |
| Sodium | 11 | 23 |
| Magnesium | 12 | 24 |
| Chlorine | 17 | 35 |
| Argon | 18 | 40 |

1. Complete the table with the three particle counts for each.
2. Draw all seven diagrams. Shells hold 2, 8, 8.
3. Which have a **full** outer shell? What does that predict about reactivity?
4. Sodium loses one electron; chlorine gains one. Draw both **ions**, and give
   the charge of each.
5. Magnesium-24 and magnesium-26 are both magnesium. What differs, and what
   stays the same?

> [!tip] Check yourself
> A neutral atom has equal protons and electrons. If yours does not, either it
> is an ion — say so — or you have made an arithmetic error.
