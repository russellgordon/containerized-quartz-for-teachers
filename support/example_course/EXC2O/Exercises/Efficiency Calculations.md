$$
\text{efficiency} = \frac{E_{\text{useful}}}{E_{\text{total}}} \times 100\%
\qquad P = VI \qquad E = Pt
$$

1. A motor is supplied $500\ \mathrm{J}$ and delivers $425\ \mathrm{J}$ of
   motion. Find its efficiency and the energy wasted.
2. A bulb converts $1\,200\ \mathrm{J}$ into $60\ \mathrm{J}$ of light.
   Efficiency? What became of the rest?
3. A $1500\ \mathrm{W}$ kettle runs for 3 minutes. How much energy does it
   use, in joules and in kilowatt-hours?
4. At $0.13\ \mathrm{\$/kWh}$, what does question 3 cost?
5. A 60 W incandescent bulb and a 9 W LED give the same light. Over 1000 hours,
   how much energy does each use, and what is the saving in dollars?
6. A solar panel receives $1000\ \mathrm{W/m^2}$ and produces
   $200\ \mathrm{W/m^2}$. Efficiency? Where does the rest go?

## Answers

1. $85\%$, $75\ \mathrm{J}$ wasted
2. $5\%$; the rest becomes heat
3. $270\,000\ \mathrm{J} = 0.075\ \mathrm{kWh}$
4. About $\$0.01$
5. $60\ \mathrm{kWh}$ versus $9\ \mathrm{kWh}$; about $\$6.63$ saved
6. $20\%$; the rest is reflected or becomes heat
