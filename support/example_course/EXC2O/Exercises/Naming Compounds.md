## Ionic: metal + non-metal

The metal keeps its name; the non-metal takes the ending **-ide**. No prefixes.

- $\mathrm{NaCl}$ — sodium chloride
- $\mathrm{MgO}$ — magnesium oxide
- $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$ — calcium chloride

## Molecular: non-metal + non-metal

Prefixes on both, except *mono-* is dropped on the first.

- $\mathrm{CO}$ — carbon monoxide
- $\mathrm{N_2O_4}$ — dinitrogen tetroxide

## Practice — name these

1. $\mathrm{KBr}$
2. $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$
3. $\mathrm{SO_2}$
4. $\mathrm{PCl_5}$
5. $\mathrm{Li_2S}$

## Practice — write the formula

6. Calcium fluoride
7. Sodium oxide
8. Carbon tetrachloride
9. Diphosphorus pentoxide
10. Aluminium chloride

## Answers

1. potassium bromide 2. aluminium oxide 3. sulfur dioxide
4. phosphorus pentachloride 5. lithium sulfide
6. $\mathrm{CaF_2}$ 7. $\mathrm{Na_2O}$ 8. $\mathrm{CCl_4}$
9. $\mathrm{P_2O_5}$ 10. $\mathrm{AlCl_3}$

> [!warning] Decide the type first
> Prefixes on an ionic compound is the single most common error. Check whether
> the first element is a metal **before** you write anything down.
