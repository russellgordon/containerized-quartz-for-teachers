Science deals with the very large and the very small, so we write numbers in a
form that keeps them readable.

$$
3\,450\,000 = 3.45 \times 10^{6} \qquad 0.000\,072 = 7.2 \times 10^{-5}
$$

## Practice

Write in scientific notation:

1. $149\,600\,000\ \mathrm{km}$ (Earth to Sun)
2. $0.000\,000\,000\,1\ \mathrm{m}$ (roughly an atom)
3. $8\,640\ \mathrm{s}$

Write as ordinary numbers:

4. $6.02 \times 10^{23}$
5. $1.6 \times 10^{-19}$

Convert:

6. $2.5\ \mathrm{km}$ to metres
7. $450\ \mathrm{mL}$ to litres
8. $3\ \mathrm{h}$ to seconds
9. $1500\ \mathrm{W}$ to kilowatts

> [!tip] Significant digits, briefly
> Your answer cannot be more precise than your least precise measurement. If you
> measured $12.4\ \mathrm{cm}$ and $3\ \mathrm{cm}$, do not report
> $37.2\ \mathrm{cm^2}$ — report $40\ \mathrm{cm^2}$.

## Answers

1. $1.496 \times 10^{8}\ \mathrm{km}$
2. $1 \times 10^{-10}\ \mathrm{m}$
3. $8.64 \times 10^{3}\ \mathrm{s}$
4. $602\,000\,000\,000\,000\,000\,000\,000$
5. $0.000\,000\,000\,000\,000\,000\,16$
6. $2500\ \mathrm{m}$
7. $0.450\ \mathrm{L}$
8. $10\,800\ \mathrm{s}$
9. $1.5\ \mathrm{kW}$
