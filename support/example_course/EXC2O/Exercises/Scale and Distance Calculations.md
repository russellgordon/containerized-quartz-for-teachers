Use $1\ \mathrm{AU} = 1.50 \times 10^{8}\ \mathrm{km}$ and
$1\ \mathrm{ly} = 9.46 \times 10^{12}\ \mathrm{km}$.

1. Jupiter is $5.20\ \mathrm{AU}$ from the Sun. How many kilometres?
2. Light takes 8 min 20 s to reach Earth. Show that this gives 1 AU.
3. Proxima Centauri is $4.24\ \mathrm{ly}$ away. Convert to kilometres, then
   to AU.
4. Voyager 1 travels about $17\ \mathrm{km/s}$. How long would it take to
   reach Proxima Centauri?
5. Andromeda is 2.5 million light-years away. How old is the light you see from
   it? What were humans doing then?
6. On a model where the Sun is a 24 cm basketball, how far away is Neptune
   ($4.50 \times 10^{9}\ \mathrm{km}$)?

## Answers

1. $7.80 \times 10^{8}\ \mathrm{km}$
2. $(3.00 \times 10^{5})(500) = 1.50 \times 10^{8}\ \mathrm{km}$ ✓
3. $4.01 \times 10^{13}\ \mathrm{km} \approx 268\,000\ \mathrm{AU}$
4. About $75\,000$ years
5. 2.5 million years — early *Homo* was making stone tools
6. About $777\ \mathrm{m}$
