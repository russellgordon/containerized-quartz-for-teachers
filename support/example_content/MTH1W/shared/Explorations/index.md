These are the problems we stand up for — literally. You work them in
random groups of three at the vertical whiteboards, and they arrive
*before* anyone teaches you a technique. That is by design: the
thinking you invent at the board is the mathematics this course is
made of. Being stuck is part of the plan, not a malfunction —
[[Getting Unstuck]] is the companion page for exactly that moment.

| Exploration | The question, roughly |
| --- | --- |
| [[The Painted Cube]] | Dip a cube in paint — what do the pieces say? |
| [[The Locker Problem]] | A hundred lockers toggle — which stay open? |
| [[How Big Is a Million]] | How big is a million, really? |
| [[Which Phone Plan]] | When does the cheap plan stop being cheap? |
| [[Bungee Drop]] | How many elastics make the perfect drop? |
| [[The Best Box]] | One sheet of card — how much can a box hold? |

None of these pages contains a full solution — on purpose. Answers get
built the way mathematics actually gets built: together, in marker.
