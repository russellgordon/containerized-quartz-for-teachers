These questions follow [[Fractions, Decimals, and Percents]] — the
thinking behind them was built in class during [[Fraction Talks]] and
[[Would You Rather]]. Attempt each one before expanding its answer.

## Questions

1. Without converting to decimals, decide which is larger:
   $\frac{3}{5}$ or $\frac{5}{8}$. Explain how you know.
2. Evaluate $\frac{2}{3} + \frac{3}{4}$.
3. A recipe needs $2\frac{1}{2}$ cups of flour. You have $1\frac{2}{3}$
   cups. How much more do you need?
4. A jacket is $30\%$ off. At the register, a coupon takes a further
   $20\%$ off the sale price. Explain why the total discount is *not*
   $50\%$, and find what it actually is.
5. **Find the error.** Avery writes
   $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{5}$. What went wrong, and
   what is the correct sum?
6. The temperature drops $1\frac{1}{2}$ °C every hour for
   $2\frac{1}{2}$ hours. Write the total change as a single signed
   mixed number.
7. **Challenge.** Find the number exactly halfway between
   $\frac{1}{3}$ and $\frac{1}{2}$, and explain your method.

## Answers

> [!success]- Answer 1
> Use a common denominator of 40: $\frac{3}{5} = \frac{24}{40}$ and
> $\frac{5}{8} = \frac{25}{40}$ — so $\frac{5}{8}$ is larger.

> [!success]- Answer 2
> Twelfths fit both: $\frac{8}{12} + \frac{9}{12} = \frac{17}{12}$,
> which is $1\frac{5}{12}$.

> [!success]- Answer 3
> $2\frac{1}{2} - 1\frac{2}{3} = \frac{15}{6} - \frac{10}{6}
> = \frac{5}{6}$ of a cup more.

> [!success]- Answer 4
> The second discount acts on the *sale* price, not the original. You
> pay $70\%$, then $80\%$ of that: $0.70 \times 0.80 = 0.56$, so you
> pay $56\%$ — a total discount of $44\%$, not $50\%$.

> [!success]- Answer 5
> Avery added tops and bottoms separately, but halves and thirds are
> different-sized pieces. In sixths: $\frac{3}{6} + \frac{2}{6} =
> \frac{5}{6}$. A sense check agrees: the sum must exceed one half.

> [!success]- Answer 6
> Each hourly change is $-1\frac{1}{2}$, so the total is
> $-\frac{3}{2} \times \frac{5}{2} = -\frac{15}{4} = -3\frac{3}{4}$ °C.

> [!success]- Answer 7
> Halfway means the average: half of
> $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{6}$ is $\frac{5}{12}$ — one
> twelfth from each neighbour. Balanced.
