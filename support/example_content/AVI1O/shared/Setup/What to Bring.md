A sketchbook, something to draw with, and clothes you do not mind
wrecking. That is the whole list, and here is what it means in practice.

| Bring | Why |
| --- | --- |
| A sketchbook, at least A5, unlined | Every class starts and ends in it — [[The Sketchbook]] |
| Pencils: an HB, a 2B, and a softer one if you have it | One hardness gives you one kind of line, and [[Making Marks]] needs several |
| A plastic eraser and a sharpener | A worn eraser smears; a blunt pencil hides what your hand is doing |
| A black fineliner or ballpoint | For the ink work in [[Drawing]], and for annotating trials |
| Clothes that can get ruined, or an old shirt to pull over | Printing ink and acrylic do not come out, ever |
| A bag your sketchbook fits in flat | A curled sketchbook photographs badly for [[The Portfolio]] |

## What the school provides

Everything else. Paint, brushes, ink, brayers, papers, boards, lino,
carving tools, clay, glue, the press, and the safety equipment that goes
with them. You do not buy paint for this course, and you do not buy
anything for [[The Media Trials]] — the point of that task is to try
materials the school owns before you decide which ones you like.

## Nobody has to buy expensive materials

The good work in this room is not made by whoever spent the most at the
art store. A soft pencil and cheap paper, used seriously, beats an
untouched set of markers every time. If the basic kit above is a problem
for any reason, come and tell me privately — there is a supply in the
cupboard for exactly that, and no explanation is required.

## Leave at home

Anything you would be upset to lose or splash. Loose sleeves, dangling
sleeves in particular, come off or get rolled before the ink comes out,
and long hair gets tied back near the press and the carving tools —
[[Materials and Safety]] explains which tools that rule is really about.

> [!tip] Keep a spare set at school
> The commonest reason a student cannot start is that the sketchbook is at
> home. A second pencil and eraser in a locker fixes a whole semester of
> that, and [[Getting Help]] is where to ask if a period has already gone
> that way.
