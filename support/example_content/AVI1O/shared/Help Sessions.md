Times the studio is open and I am in it. No appointment needed — bring
the work, or a photograph of it.

| When | Where | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday, lunch | The art room | Working on a piece with space and materials |
| Wednesday, after school until 4:30 | The art room | Longer studio time, and messier processes |
| Any morning before period 1, by arrangement | The art room | Quick questions, marks, deadlines |

The two weeks before [[The Exhibition]] are busy — come early in that
stretch rather than the day before installation.

> [!tip] Bring the actual work
> A photograph on a phone loses the surface, and the surface is usually
> what we need to look at. If you cannot carry it, photograph it well —
> see [[Photographing Your Work]].

If none of these times work, say so and we will find one. See
[[Getting Help]] for the other ways to reach me.
