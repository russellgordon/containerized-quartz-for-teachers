rather than decorative.

| Where | What is in it |

## Three things worth knowing

1. **Links go both ways.** At the bottom of every page is a
   **Backlinks** list showing which pages link *to* it. Land on
   [[Balancing Equations]] and you can see every class and every
   investigation that used it.
2. **Hover to peek.** Hovering over a link previews the page in a small
   window without leaving the one you are on. Useful for checking one
   definition mid-paragraph.
3. **Search is fast.** Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> — or
   <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> — and start typing. It searches the text
   of every page, not just the titles.

## The order pages get written in

Concept pages assume you have already met the idea. That is on purpose:
in this course you do the investigation first, we compare what
different groups actually observed, and only then does the idea get its
name and its clean statement. So a concept page will often open by
referring to the lab you have just finished. If you are reading ahead
and it feels like it starts in the middle, that is why.

> [!tip] The habit that gets you unstuck fastest
> Lost on a class page? Follow its link to the concept. Lost on a
> concept page? Scroll to its backlinks and find the class where we did
> it, then read what we actually did that day. The two directions
> between them answer most "when did we cover this" questions in under
> a minute.
