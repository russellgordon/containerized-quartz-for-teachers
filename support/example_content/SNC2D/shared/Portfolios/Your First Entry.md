Write this in the first week, before the course has had a chance to
change your mind about anything. Roughly 300 words, answering four
things.

1. **Where you are starting.** What do you already like or dislike
   about science, and what happened to make you feel that way? Name the
   actual event if you can — a class, a lab, a thing somebody said.
2. **What you believe about how science works.** One sentence. Not the
   textbook answer, the one you actually hold.
3. **What you want to be able to explain** by the end of the course.
   Something specific, not "chemistry".
4. **Your worry.** One thing you expect to find hard. Naming it now is
   what lets you notice, later, the week it stops being hard.

> [!note] Honesty is the only requirement
> "I have never liked science and I am taking it because I need the
> credit" is a perfectly good first entry. It is also, reliably, the
> entry that is most interesting to re-read in the last week of the
> semester — which is exactly what [[Showing Growth]] asks you to do.

Question 2 is the new one this year, and it is worth taking seriously.
Come back to it at the end and see whether you would still write the
same sentence.
