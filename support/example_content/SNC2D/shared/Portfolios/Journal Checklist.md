What I check for at the end of the semester, so you can check for it
first.

## Contents

- [ ] [[Your First Entry]] present, from the first week
- [ ] An entry for each of the four investigations you ran
- [ ] Every one of those entries contains a prediction that was
      recorded **before** the measurement
- [ ] At least one entry about work that went badly, and what you did
      next
- [ ] Notes from at least two discussions, saying what moved
- [ ] A [[Showing Growth]] comparison from mid-semester
- [ ] [[Final Reflection]] completed
- [ ] Dates on everything, in order, readable by a stranger

## How it is assessed

| Criterion | What I am looking for |
| --- | --- |
| Selection | A range, not only the entries where you looked good |
| Evidence | Numbers, units, and instrument limits — not adjectives |
| Reasoning | Mechanism attempted, not just the pattern described |
| Honesty | Limitations named by you before I have to find them |
| Growth | Change shown with two pieces side by side, not asserted |
| Care | Organised well enough that a reader can follow it cold |

The reasoning row is the one that separates this course from last year.
"The temperature rose faster in the dark container" describes a
pattern. "The temperature rose faster because the dark surface absorbed
more of the incoming radiation and re-radiated it as infrared, which the
gas in the container absorbs" attempts a mechanism — and can be wrong in
an interesting way, which is worth more than being vaguely right.

> [!tip] The five-minute audit
> Open three entries at random. If none of them contains a number with a
> unit, that is your gap. If none of them contains a sentence starting
> "I was wrong about", that is your other one.
