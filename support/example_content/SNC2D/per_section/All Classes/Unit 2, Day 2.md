Twenty labels from the chemical cupboard, and no rules handed out. Find
the pattern in the names yourselves.

## Agenda

1. Sort the twenty labels; work out how the names are built
2. Predict the name on a bottle we have not shown you yet
3. Name it: [[Chemical Formulas and Names]] — where your rule held, and broke
4. Practise until the naming is automatic: [[Formulas and Names Practice]]
