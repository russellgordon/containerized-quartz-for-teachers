A lamp, three pinholes, and a card. Your job is to make the beam do
something that straight lines cannot account for.

## Agenda

1. Line the pinholes up, then try to get light around a corner
2. What your evidence proves, and what it only suggests
3. Name it: the ray model — useful, and not a claim about what light is
4. Tools you will want this unit: [[What This Site Can Do]]

## Things to do before our next class

- [ ] Journal: your sharpest evidence that light travels in straight lines.
- [ ] Predict, with a number: what does a mirror do to the angle of a beam?
