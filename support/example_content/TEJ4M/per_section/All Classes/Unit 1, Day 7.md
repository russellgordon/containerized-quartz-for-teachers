## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Which One Doesn't Belong]] — four resistors, one value
2. Bench: twenty nominally identical resistors, one meter, one table.
   Predict the spread in writing, then measure all twenty and plot the
   class's results on the board
3. The batch is not the label. Open the datasheet for the part you
   just measured and find its tolerance, its temperature coefficient,
   and its power rating: [[Reading a Datasheet Like an Engineer]]
4. Name it: [[Component Selection and Tolerances]] — how a value you
   chose becomes a range you are obliged to design for
5. Back to your block diagram: choose real parts for two blocks, each
   with a reason and a number attached

## Things to do before our next class

- [ ] Journal: the widest gap you found between a marked value and a
      measured one, and what that gap would do to your circuit.
