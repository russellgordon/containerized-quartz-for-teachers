## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Read the Waveform]] — a step response, and what its
   overshoot says about the controller that produced it
2. Bench: [[Close the Loop]]. Predict the overshoot and the period of
   the oscillation in writing before the heater is switched on
3. Name it: [[Feedback and Control Systems]] — setpoint, error,
   actuator, and the delay that decides whether a loop is stable
4. Retune, then prove the retune: two traces, one claim, and a
   sentence saying which parameter you changed and why
5. Practise: [[Control Systems Practice]]
