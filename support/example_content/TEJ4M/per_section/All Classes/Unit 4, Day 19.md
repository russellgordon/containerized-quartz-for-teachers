## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Read the Schematic]] — the standards round: which
   symbols are conventions, and which ones are requirements
2. Bench audit: walk the shop with a clipboard and judge our own
   semester's work against a written standard. Findings go on the
   board, unsigned, and every bench contributes at least one
3. Name it: [[Standards and Professional Practice]] — who writes the
   rules, who enforces them, and what a signature actually commits you
   to
4. Bring back [[When Good Enough Is Not Safe]] with a year of design
   decisions behind you. The answer should be harder now, not easier
5. Log the clause of our own agreement your bench breaks most often
