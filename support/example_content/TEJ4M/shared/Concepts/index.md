These pages are the settled version of what the bench already showed you.
Read them after the lab, with a measurement in your log and a question
about why it came out that way — never instead of the lab.

Grade 11 asked whether your circuit worked, and at what current. This
year asks three harder questions of everything you build: will it still
work in a year, could somebody else maintain it from your documents, and
what does it do when it fails? That is why almost every page here ends up
somewhere the curriculum puts it — you are expected to
[[A3.1|describe specifications in accurate technical terminology]] and to
[[B3.1|use a design process to design circuits]], not to copy a working
one off a page.
