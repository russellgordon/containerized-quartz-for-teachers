Pages about the site itself — how it is arranged, what it can do, and
how to write for it.

- [[How This Site Is Organised]] — the folders, and why the links
  between them matter more than the folders do.
- [[What This Site Can Do]] — every feature a page can use, with the
  source shown for each one. The working reference for teachers, and
  for anyone curious about how the notes are made.
- [[Writing About Technology]] — measurements, specifications, design
  defence, and documentation the next engineer can actually work from.

If you used the Grade 10 or Grade 11 site, all three names are familiar
and the site works the same way. The page that has genuinely changed
again is [[Writing About Technology]], because the standard has. Two
years ago a claim needed a measurement. Last year a measurement needed
units and conditions. This year a **design decision needs its rejected
alternative** — and a requirement needs a test that could fail it.
