One page per class, newest at the top of your teacher's list: the agenda we
followed and anything to do before next time. If you were away, start here —
then try the thinking task before reading anything else about it. The page
holds the plan; the discoveries happen at the boards.

Our semester runs in four units:

- **Unit 1 — Crossing Lines**: what an intersection means, then substitution and elimination, ending in [[Break-Even]].
- **Unit 2 — Geometry on the Grid**: midpoints, lengths, circles, and proof, argued through [[The Quadrilateral Case File]].
- **Unit 3 — The Parabola**: patterns, factoring, vertex form, and the formula, modelled in [[The Perfect Arc]].
- **Unit 4 — Triangles and Heights**: similarity and trigonometry — [[Inaccessible Heights]], presented at [[The Math Symposium]].
