A dance studio only works if everyone in it feels able to try something
badly in front of other people. That takes agreement, not luck.

## How we treat each other

- **No commentary on anyone's body.** Not size, not shape, not
  flexibility, not weight — not as insult and not as compliment. This is
  the rule that gets broken most often by accident.
- **Watch generously.** People are showing you unfinished work. React to
  it the way you would want yours received.
- **Feedback goes to the work.** "The ending felt sudden" is useful.
  Anything about the dancer rather than the dancing is not — see
  [[Showing and Feedback]].
- **Laugh with, never at.** Falling over is part of this.

## Touch and partner work

Dance involves contact, and contact requires consent every time.

- **Ask before you touch someone**, including to correct or support
  them, and accept "no" without needing a reason.
- **I will ask before I make a hands-on correction**, and you may
  decline; I will find another way to say it.
- **Tell your partner what you are about to do** before you take weight
  or lift — [[Partner Warm-Ups]].
- **Nobody is required to partner with anyone.** Tell me privately if a
  pairing does not work for you.

## Space, and other people's dances

- Cross behind someone who is working, never through their space.
- If you learn a dance that belongs to a community, learn where it comes
  from too, and find out what that community says about sharing it —
  [[Does a Dance Belong to a Culture?]].

## Privacy

We film in this course, for your portfolio. Video of you belongs to you:
it is not posted, shared, or sent anywhere without your say-so, and the
same goes for video of anyone else. If you would rather not be filmed,
tell me and we will arrange it.

> [!warning] If something in this course is hard for a real reason
> Injury, illness, an eating disorder, a body you are at war with, a
> reason you do not want to be looked at — tell me beforehand or
> afterwards. You do not owe me the reason, and it will not change how
> you are marked.
