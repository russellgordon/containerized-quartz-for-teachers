Ask early. In a studio course the small problems — a step you cannot
get, a group that is stuck, a body that hurts — get expensive quickly if
they wait.

## Ways to reach me

| How | When | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before or after class, in the studio | Every day | A step, a phrase, a quick question |
| [[Help Sessions]] | The posted times | Working with me in the room, with space |
| A booked conversation | Any week | Anything needing more than five minutes |
| Email | Any time; replies on school days | Something you want in writing |

## What helps most

- **Show me rather than describe it.** Thirty seconds on your feet beats
  five minutes of explaining.
- **Bring the video.** If you filmed it, we can both see what happened.
- **Name what you have tried.** "I've tried it slower and it still
  falls apart at the turn" gets a real answer immediately.

## For groups

Come as a group and bring your notes. Most group problems are a decision
nobody made — who is deciding, or what "finished" means — and
[[Devising in a Group]] is where that gets sorted.

## For anything physical

Pain, dizziness, a joint that does not feel right, or worry about eating
and your body: tell me, and I will help you find the right person. That
is a professional conversation, not a favour, and it will not change how
you are marked.

## Beyond this course

If something outside class is making school hard, say so, to me or to
guidance. It works better when it starts early, and you do not have to
explain why.
