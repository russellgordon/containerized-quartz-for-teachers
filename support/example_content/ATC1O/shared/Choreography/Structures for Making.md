Once you have a phrase, the next question is what to do with it — and the
answer is almost never "make another five phrases." Choreographers get an
enormous amount out of very little material by putting one phrase through a
structure. Five structures will carry you through this whole course.

| Structure | What you do | What it is good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canon | The same phrase, started at staggered times | Filling a stage |
| Retrograde | The phrase backwards, last move first | Making material strange |
| Motif and development | One small idea, varied again | Unity you can see |
| Accumulation | Move 1; then 1–2; then 1–2–3, onward | Building pressure |
| Call and response | One states, another answers | Relationship and contrast |

## Choosing one on purpose

The mistake is picking the structure you think looks most sophisticated. Ask
instead what the dance needs. If your phrase is strong but the stage looks
empty, canon. If the piece needs to feel like something coming undone,
retrograde. If the audience keeps missing your best three seconds, motif and
development will show it to them four more times until they see it.

You can stack structures — a canon of an accumulation is a real thing — but
do that after each one works alone.

## What each one costs

Retrograde is far harder to perform than to plan. Weight shifts run
backwards, which sometimes makes a transition genuinely impossible rather
than merely awkward, and momentum you relied on disappears. Test it on the
floor the day you decide on it, not the week before you show.

Canon lives or dies on counting. If the group cannot hold a count without
music, the canon will collapse into a blur within two repetitions.
Accumulation grows long very fast — a twelve-move accumulation is
seventy-eight moves of dancing — so keep the starting phrase tiny.

[[Choreographic Form]] sets these in the wider picture of how whole dances
are shaped, and everything here assumes you have material that survives
[[Phrase Building|the two-repetition test]]. Your
[[The Composition Study|composition study]] will need at least one of these
structures used deliberately enough that a watcher can name it.
