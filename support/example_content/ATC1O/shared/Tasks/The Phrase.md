**Shown mid-Unit 2.** Pairs. About 30 seconds.

One phrase, made together, that uses at least two elements of dance
deliberately and solves a problem you were given.

## The problem

Your pair receives a constraint — travel without turning, keep contact
throughout, use only two levels, repeat one movement four times in
different energies, or perform it twice at different speeds. The
constraint is the point: it forces choices you would not have made.

## What is marked

| Criterion | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| The elements | Two or more used deliberately, and you can say which |
| The solution | The constraint is genuinely met, not worked around |
| Construction | The phrase has a shape — it goes somewhere |
| Partnership | Both dancers contributed material, and it shows |
| Performance | Together, clean, committed |

> [!success]- What "solving it" means (click to expand)
> A pair told to travel without turning who shuffle sideways for thirty
> seconds have complied. A pair who find four ways to cover ground
> facing one direction have solved it. The difference is how many
> options you generated before choosing.

## Showing it

Phrases are shown to the class and discussed with the feedback protocol
on [[Showing and Feedback]] — describe before you judge, and speak to
the work rather than the dancer.

[[Phrase Building]] is the method, and [[Structures for Making]] is
where you will find more to try.
