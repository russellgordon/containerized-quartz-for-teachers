**Shown in the studio near the end of Unit 1.** Individual. 45–60
seconds of your own movement.

A short solo built from a vocabulary that is yours — not steps you were
taught, movement you generated.

## How it is built

1. **Choose a stimulus.** An object, a photograph, a piece of music, a
   line of text, a place. It goes in your journal with the date.
2. **Improvise from it**, three times, on three different days, and
   write down what came out — see [[Improvisation Warm-Ups]].
3. **Keep four or five movements** that feel like yours and are worth
   repeating.
4. **Order them**, decide what repeats, and fix the beginning and the
   end. A signature with no ending is unfinished.

## What is being marked

| Criterion | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Vocabulary | The movement is generated, not borrowed |
| Elements | You made deliberate choices about body, space, time, and energy |
| Repeatability | You can perform it twice the same way |
| Performance | You commit to it; it holds together from first move to last |
| Anatomy | You can name what the body is doing — joints, not just shapes |

> [!tip] Distinct beats impressive
> A signature made of three clear movements you own is worth more here
> than eight difficult ones you cannot repeat. The clearest solos in this
> unit are usually the simplest.

[[The Elements of Dance]] is the vocabulary you are choosing from, and
[[Making a Movement Vocabulary]] is how the generating works.
