## The categories

| Category | Here, that means |
| --- | --- |
| **Knowledge and Understanding** | You know the texts and the critical vocabulary |
| **Thinking** | You interpret, evaluate, and handle evidence that complicates your case |
| **Communication** | The writing is organised, cohesive, and yours |
| **Application** | You transfer the method to a text nobody taught you |

## The split

**70%** across the course; **30%** for the final evaluation —
[[Final Examination]] and the last stage of [[The Independent Study]].

Later work counts for more. That is not leniency; it is what a mark is
supposed to describe.

## The independent study, and why it is weighted where it is

It runs across five months with four checkpoints, and each checkpoint is
assessed. A student who does the whole thing in the last fortnight
produces something that reads exactly like a thing done in a fortnight,
and the checkpoints exist to make that visible early enough to fix.

## Late work

Talk to me before the deadline and we set a new one. Silence is the
problem: work that never arrives cannot be assessed.
