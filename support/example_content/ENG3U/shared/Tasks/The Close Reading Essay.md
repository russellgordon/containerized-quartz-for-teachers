> [!abstract] At a glance
> Individual · four class periods · 700 words · assessed on Thinking,
> Communication, and Knowledge

## The task

One argument about one story from [[Short Fiction]], proved from the
words on the page.

Seven hundred words is short on purpose. There is no room for plot
summary, no room for three quotations that make the same point, and no
room for an introduction that clears its throat.

## What it must contain

1. **A thesis with a mechanism** — what the story does, and how. See
   [[Thesis and Argument]].
2. **Three passages**, quoted short.
3. **Analysis longer than the evidence**, every time.
4. **One sentence acknowledging the best objection**, and one answering
   it.

## How it is built

| Period | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Pattern-finding from your annotations; claim drafted |
| 2 | Passages chosen and paraphrased; outline |
| 3 | Draft, in class |
| 4 | Workshop, then rewrite: a partner marks the weakest analysis |

## How it is marked

| Category | What I am looking for |
| --- | --- |
| Knowledge | The story is known precisely, including what it does not say |
| Thinking | The claim is arguable, the evidence supports it, the objection is real |
| Communication | 700 words that a reader follows without rereading |

> [!tip] The discipline of the word count
> Draft to 900 and cut to 700. The cutting is the assessment nobody
> writes down: what survives is what was doing work.
