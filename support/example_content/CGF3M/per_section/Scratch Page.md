## Ideas for next time

- The second shoreline visit did more work than anything else in Unit 4.
  Consider a third short visit in Unit 2, so the change is sampled three
  times rather than twice.
- The woodlot microclimate readings only work if every pair records the
  time. Two pairs did not, and their comparison was worthless. Put a
  clock check into the preparation period.
- The urban heat island measurement in the schoolyard was a five-minute
  activity that landed harder than the Lytton figure. Give it a whole
  period next year and take the readings twice, morning and afternoon.
- The land use hearing worked best where the proposal was genuinely
  live. A closed file lets students argue without cost.
