Three sites within reach of the school, each carrying evidence you cannot
get from a screen. Several tasks in this course require your own
measurements, so the field days are not optional enrichment — they are
where a good part of the course's data comes from.

- [[Fieldwork Safety]] — read before every field day, not once
- [[The Shoreline Study]] — erosion, sediment, and storm exposure
- [[The Woodlot Study]] — soils, slope, and microclimate
- [[The Farm Visit]] — the physical constraints agriculture works inside

Each page tells you what we do, what to record, and what the day feeds
into afterwards. Bring [[Your Field Notebook]] every time; what to wear
and carry is in [[What to Bring]].
