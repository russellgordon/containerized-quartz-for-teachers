Times I am in the room and free to work with you. No appointment, no
reason needed.

| When | Where | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday, lunch | Room 118 | Data you cannot find, and sources you are unsure of |
| Wednesday, after school until 4:15 | Room 118 | Drafts, mapping, and GIS with the layers open |
| Any morning before period 1, by arrangement | Room 118 | Quick questions, marks, deadlines |

The Wednesday session is the one to use for anything with a map in it —
the projector is free then and we can put your stack up on the wall.
During the culminating assessment it becomes group meeting time, so book
it rather than arriving with three other groups.

> [!tip] Bring the thing itself
> The notebook page, the spreadsheet, the map, the paragraph. Ten
> minutes with your own field data open beats half an hour describing
> what you think it showed.

If you missed a field day, come to any of these and we will sort out how
you get the measurements — there is always a way, and it works far better
in the same week than in the same month.

If none of these times work for you, say so and we will find one. The
other ways to reach me are in [[Getting Help]].
