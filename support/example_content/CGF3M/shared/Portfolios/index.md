Three collections built across the whole term rather than handed in on
one day. They are where the course's continuity lives: what you recorded
in September becomes evidence in January.

- [[Your Field Notebook]] — every field day, observation on the left and
  inference on the right
- [[A Map of Your Own]] — four maps, each with a reflection on what you
  chose to leave off
- [[Skills You Are Building]] — what this course is actually teaching
  you, and where else it is used

Keep all three going from week one. A portfolio assembled in the last
fortnight is visible from a long way off, and it is worth less to you
than to the mark.
