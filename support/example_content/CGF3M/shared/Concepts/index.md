The ideas of the course, one to a page, grouped by the unit that
uncovers them. Each is written to be read *after* the class that met the
idea — they state it cleanly so you can use it, and they are not a
textbook to read ahead in.

- [[Sharing a Watershed]]

The four units are not four separate subjects. Unit 2 uses the processes
from Unit 1 and asks what happens when they meet people; Unit 3 asks what
people do back; Unit 4 asks who that reaches. If a page in a later unit
seems to assume something, the assumption is almost always in an earlier
one on this list.
