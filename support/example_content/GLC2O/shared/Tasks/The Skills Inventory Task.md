**Unit 1. Individual. Due at the end of week three.** Two to three
pages, or an equivalent you have agreed with me.

The first assessed piece, and the foundation of everything after it. You
document what you can already do, with evidence, and you set out how you
intend to keep yourself working well over the next two years.

## What to hand in

**Part one — the inventory.** At least ten skills you can evidence,
drawn from [[The Skills Inventory]]. For each: the skill, the situation
that demonstrates it, and who could confirm it. Include at least three
that came from outside school.

**Part two — adapting and persevering.** Two situations from your own
life where something did not go the way you wanted. What you did next,
what worked, what did not, and what you would do differently. This is
the resilience half of the task, and it is assessed on the thinking, not
on how difficult the situation was.

**Part three — keeping yourself working well.** Working from
[[Looking After Yourself While You Work]], three strategies you
already use to manage pressure and stay balanced, with a sentence on
when each one actually helps. Then: name three places a person at this
school could go for support with their mental health — one person, one
service inside the school, one outside it — and say specifically how a
person would reach each.

## What this task does not ask for

Part three is about strategies and about knowing where support exists.
It does not ask you to disclose anything about your own mental health,
and nothing you write will be treated as a disclosure. If you do want to
talk about something, say so and we will talk — separately from this,
and not for marks.

## What earns the marks

| Criterion | What it looks like here |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence | Every skill attached to a situation a third person could confirm |
| Range | Skills drawn from more than one part of your life |
| Reflection | Part two shows what you learned, not just what happened |
| Practicality | Strategies you have actually used, described specifically |
| Accuracy | Part three's supports are real, current, and reachable |
| Communication | Organised, plain, and something you would show an adult |

Everything here goes into [[Your Career Portfolio]] as your first
entries, and the inventory gets reused in [[The Application]] — so write
it once, properly.
