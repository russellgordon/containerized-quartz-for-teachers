This course asks you to talk about your own life — your money, your
family's expectations, what you are worried about, what you want. That
only works if the room is safe to do it in, and a room is not safe by
accident. This is the agreement that makes it one.

## What stays in the room

What someone says about their family, their money, their marks, their
health, or their plans stays here. Not repeated at lunch, not in a group
chat, not "it wasn't a big deal". This is the one rule with no
flexibility in it.

You choose your own level. Nothing in this course requires you to share
anything about yourself that you would rather not, and a general answer
is always acceptable. Marks are for the thinking, never for the amount
disclosed.

The exception is the ordinary one, and you should know it in advance: if
I learn that someone is being harmed or is at risk of harming
themselves, I have to pass that on to the people whose job it is to
help. I will tell you when I am doing it and why.

## How we talk about plans

- **No pathway is a fallback.** Not out loud, and not in the tone.
  Someone's plan to start work at eighteen gets the same questions as
  someone's plan to apply to university.
- **Nobody's family finances are anyone's business.** Budgets get
  discussed as numbers and methods, not as circumstances.
- **Disagreement is fine, ranking is not.** "Have you looked at what
  that costs?" is a good question. "Why would you do that?" is not the
  same question.

## How we work

- Come with the thing you were asked to bring, because most periods are
  built on it.
- Do the finding-out yourself. When you cannot find something, say what
  you tried.
- Devices are for the looking-up, and the looking-up is genuinely most
  of this course.
- Work with whoever you are put with. You will not choose your
  colleagues later either.

> [!note] This agreement is not finished
> We go through it in the first week and you add to it. If something on
> this page does not describe the room you need, say so then — or say so
> later, privately, and it gets changed.
