The lab provides the machines; you provide the thinking. The list is
short.

## Every class

- [ ] Your journal or a notebook (see [[Dev Journal]] for what goes in
  it) — yes, paper; planning a program away from the keyboard is a
  professional habit, not a school one
- [ ] Something to write with
- [ ] Your school login details, memorised — class time spent on
  password resets is class time gone

## Sometimes, and I'll say when

- Headphones or earbuds, for days with video or audio work — one ear
  only when a partner is talking.
- Your own device is welcome for [[Setting Up Python]] at home, but
  never required: everything in this course runs on the lab machines
  and on free tools.

> [!warning] The phone rule
> Phones live in bags during build time. The device in front of you is
> already a computer; the one in your pocket is a distraction machine
> wearing the same costume. Filming or photographing anyone's screen or
> work without their okay breaks [[Our Classroom Norms]].

Forgot something? Borrow from the front desk, return it at the bell, and
nobody will make it a thing. If gear or connectivity at home is ever a
genuine barrier, tell me quietly — see [[Getting Help]] — and we will
sort it out.
