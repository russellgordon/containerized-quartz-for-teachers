Each page keeps the expectation's official **code** — `A2.3`, `C2.6`,
and so on — and every lesson, exploration, and task in this course links
back to the codes it addresses. The codes are also tags, so every page
tagged `#C2` can be gathered in one place.

## How this course reads them

Strand A — computational thinking, technology and society, careers and
connections — is practised *inside* the learning of the other two
strands: every program you plan, every issue you debate, every artifact
you build. Strand B is the world of hardware, software, and innovation
around you; strand C is programming itself.

## Why the codes matter more than they look

A code is a shared address. When a student asks "why are we doing
this?", the answer is a link. When a department writes a course outline,
or a teacher takes over a class partway through, the codes are what make
one person's planning legible to another.
