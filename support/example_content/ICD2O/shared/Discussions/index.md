Technology likes to look inevitable — as if phones, feeds, and
algorithms simply *happened*, like weather. They did not. Every system
you touch today is a pile of decisions somebody made: what to collect,
whom to design for, which tasks to automate. Decisions have reasons,
and reasons can be argued with — so in this course, we argue, on
purpose and on the record.

One rule governs all of them, straight from [[Our Classroom Norms]]:
we argue with ideas, never at people. Come ready to change your mind —
that is what the arguing is for.
