## The idea

Water moves continuously between oceans, atmosphere, land, and living things,
driven almost entirely by energy from the Sun.

| Process | What happens | Energy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evaporation | Liquid → gas from open water | Absorbed |
| Transpiration | Water vapour leaves plant leaves | Absorbed |
| Condensation | Gas → liquid, forming cloud droplets | Released |
| Precipitation | Droplets grow heavy and fall | — |
| Infiltration | Water soaks into the ground | — |
| Runoff | Water flows over the surface into rivers | — |

> [!tip] Watch where the energy goes
> Evaporation *absorbs* energy — which is why sweating cools you. Condensation
> *releases* it, which is why hurricanes are so powerful: they run on the heat
> released as enormous volumes of vapour condense.

## Why pavement matters

Cover ground with asphalt and you replace infiltration with runoff. The water
arrives at the creek in minutes instead of days, all at once, carrying road salt
and oil with it. That is the mechanism behind most urban flooding.
