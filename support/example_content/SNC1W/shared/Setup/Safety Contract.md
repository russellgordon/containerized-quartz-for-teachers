Before your first investigation, you and a parent or guardian must both sign
this. No signature, no lab.

## I agree to

- [ ] Wear safety goggles whenever anyone in the room is doing lab work
- [ ] Follow instructions exactly, and ask when I am unsure
- [ ] Never eat, drink, or taste anything in the lab
- [ ] Waft odours toward me rather than inhaling directly
- [ ] Report **every** spill, break, cut, and burn immediately
- [ ] Tie back long hair and secure loose clothing
- [ ] Know where the eyewash, safety shower, fire extinguisher, blanket, and
      exits are before I need them
- [ ] Leave my station cleaner than I found it
- [ ] Never take chemicals or equipment out of the room

## Conduct that ends lab privileges

Horseplay, ignoring instructions, or interfering with someone else's safety.
You will complete the work as a written assignment instead, and we will talk
with home.

> [!danger] The one that matters most
> **Tell me when something goes wrong, immediately.** A small spill handled
> right away is nothing. The same spill hidden for ten minutes is an incident
> report. You will never be in trouble for reporting an accident.
