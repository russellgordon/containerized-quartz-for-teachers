## Purpose

Compare indicators of water quality from several sources and decide which
supports the healthiest ecosystem.

> [!danger] Safety
> Treat every water sample as contaminated: gloves on, no tasting, wash hands
> afterwards. Dispose of samples down the drain with plenty of running water.

## Materials

- Water samples: tap, rainwater or pond, water with a pinch of fertiliser,
  water with a drop of dish soap
- pH strips, dissolved oxygen test kit, turbidity tube, thermometer

## Procedure

1. Label four sample cups. Do not open more than one at a time.
2. Measure temperature first — it changes fastest once the sample is out.
3. Measure pH, then dissolved oxygen, then turbidity.
4. Record everything, including anything unexpected about smell or colour.

> [!note] What "good" looks like
> Most freshwater fish need dissolved oxygen above about $5\ \mathrm{mg/L}$
> and a pH between $6.5$ and $8.2$. Trout are fussier than carp — tolerance
> ranges differ by species.

## Analysis

1. Rank the four samples from most to least able to support fish. Justify with
   numbers.
2. The fertiliser sample models farm runoff. Explain, using
   [[The Nitrogen Cycle]], why adding nutrients can *lower* dissolved oxygen.
3. Name one thing your test could not detect that would still matter.
