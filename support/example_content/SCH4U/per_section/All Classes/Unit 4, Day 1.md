The sealed tube goes brown, and then it stops going brown. There is still
colourless gas in there. Nothing has run out.

## Agenda

1. Watch the tube in warm water and in cold; record what changes and what
   stubbornly does not
2. Argue whether anything is still happening inside, and design a test
3. Name it: [[Dynamic Equilibrium]] — read it after the argument, not before
4. Equal rates, not equal amounts — say the difference out loud, in your
   own words, before you leave

## Things to do before our next class

- [ ] Read [[Dynamic Equilibrium]] and write down the misconception it names.
- [ ] Journal: the test you proposed for "still happening", and its flaw.
