A solid sitting under its own saturated solution is at equilibrium too,
and today you write that expression yourself rather than copying it.

## Agenda

1. Add a common ion to a saturated solution and watch what appears
2. Write the expression from first principles — the solid is left out, so
   argue why leaving it out is not cheating
3. Check your version against [[Dynamic Equilibrium]] and against
   [[Reading an Equilibrium Table]]
4. Consolidation: the questions this unit can now answer that Grade 11 could not
5. Last set of [[Equilibrium Practice]], mixed and unlabelled
