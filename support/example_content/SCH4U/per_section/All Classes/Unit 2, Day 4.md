## Agenda

1. Investigation: [[Identifying an Unknown Organic Compound]] — in the
   fume hood, well away from every flame, and waft rather than sniff
2. Sort your unknowns by behaviour before you attach any names to them
3. Name it: [[Functional Groups]] — the group is the part that reacts
4. [[Functional Groups at a Glance]] beside you while you sort
5. [[Writing About Chemistry]] — record what you saw, not what you wanted
