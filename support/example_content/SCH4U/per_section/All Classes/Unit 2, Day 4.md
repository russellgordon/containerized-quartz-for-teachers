Short molecules go in and something long and stringy comes out. You will
watch it happen before we write a single equation about it.

## Agenda

1. Demonstration: a thread drawn continuously from the boundary between
   two solutions — watch closely, then try to explain it
2. Propose what has to be happening at the ends of those molecules
3. Name it: [[Organic Reactions]] — addition, substitution, and the rest
4. [[Polymers]] — one repeating unit doing all of the work
5. Back to [[Functional Groups]]: which group made this reaction possible?

## Things to do before our next class

      feature you will look for next time.
