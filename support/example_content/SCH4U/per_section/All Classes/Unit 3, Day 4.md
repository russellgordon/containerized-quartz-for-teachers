end of the hour the room owns the list, not the textbook.

## Agenda

1. Investigation: [[Factors That Change a Rate]] — one variable, kept clean
2. Post your times against your variable on the board
3. Read the whole board — which factor did the most, and what surprised you?
4. Name it: [[Rates of Reaction]] — a rate is a slope, not a stopwatch value
5. Where your variable stopped mattering, and why that is worth recording
