Two acids at the same concentration give two very different pH readings.
Concentration is clearly not the whole story any more.

## Agenda

1. Measure the pH of several dilute acids and bases prepared at equal
   concentration, eye protection on throughout
2. Rank them, then propose what differs between the top and the bottom
3. Name it: [[Acids and Bases]] — extent of ionisation, and $K_w$
4. [[Working with Logarithms in Chemistry]] — why pH is a logarithm at all
5. Practise: [[Acids and Bases Practice]]
