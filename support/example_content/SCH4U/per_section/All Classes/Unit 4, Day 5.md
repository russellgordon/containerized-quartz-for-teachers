Add acid drop by drop to water and the pH collapses. Add the same drops
to another mixture and almost nothing happens for a long while.

## Agenda

1. Titrate into water and into the mixture, drop by drop, plotting as you go
2. Compare the two curves and mark where each one turns steepest
3. Name it: [[Buffers and Titration Curves]] — the pair doing the work
4. Task launch: [[The Buffer Design]] — a target pH and a defence of it
5. If the calculation stalls on you: [[Getting Unstuck]]

## Things to do before our next class

- [ ] Choose your target pH for [[The Buffer Design]] and justify it.
- [ ] Journal: the point on your curve you would most want to remeasure,
      and the reason it bothers you.
