Invent a name for a structure so that a group who cannot see it can
rebuild it exactly. That is the whole problem naming exists to solve.

## Agenda

1. Name four structures your own way; hand only the names to another group
2. Rebuild what comes back to you — the failures are the interesting part
3. Compare your invented rules with [[Naming Organic Compounds]]
4. Keep [[Functional Groups at a Glance]] open; the suffixes matter here
5. Practise: [[Organic Naming Practice]], names to structures and back
