Everything about how this course runs: the shape of a unit, what to
bring, how marks are put together, how to get help, and the safety
agreement that has to be signed before you can do any lab work.

Read [[Safety in the Lab]] first. It is the only page here with a
deadline attached to it — no signature, no lab. It is also genuinely
longer than the Grade 11 version, because this course handles flammable
solvents, concentrated bases, and cells that produce hydrogen, and none
of those were in last year's room.

Then [[How Chemistry Class Works]], which explains why the
investigations come before the explanations, and what changes now that
the question has moved from *how much* to *why that much and no
further*.
