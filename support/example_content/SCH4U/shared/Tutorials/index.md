How to do the things this course keeps asking of you — the label, the
calorimeter, the burette, the voltmeter, the report, and the logarithms
that arrive in Unit 4 and never quite leave.

Come back to these. Nobody remembers on first hearing why the digits
before the decimal point in a pH are not significant, and that is
exactly why it is written down.

Two are worth reading before your first lab day:
[[Lab Safety and WHMIS]], which has genuinely more in it than last
year's version because this course handles genuinely more, and
[[Measuring Well]]. The one students return to most across the semester
is [[Working with Logarithms in Chemistry]].
