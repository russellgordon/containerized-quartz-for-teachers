**Launched mid-Unit 2, due at the end of it.** Pairs.

One Canadian resource, followed from where it sits in the ground, the
water, or the forest to the argument about whether we should be taking
it.

## The inquiry

You write the question yourself, and that is the first thing marked. A
good one is answerable, specific, and genuinely open — see
[[The Geographic Inquiry Process]].

| Weak question | Stronger version |
| --- | --- |
| Is mining bad? | Should the proposed mine at X proceed, and on whose terms? |
| Why is water important? | Who decides how the water in this watershed is allocated, and how well does that work? |

## What you produce

- **The resource**, classified — renewable, non-renewable, or flow —
  and where it is found, with a map showing why it is there.
- **How it is taken and moved**: extraction or harvesting method, the
  route to market, and what the method costs environmentally.
- **The argument**, from at least three perspectives that genuinely
  disagree — including, where the resource sits on treaty land or
  traditional territory, the position of the First Nation, Métis, or
  Inuit communities involved, in their own published words.
- **Your answer** to your question, with the strongest objection to it
  answered.

## How it is marked

- **The question** — worth answering, and answered.
- **Perspectives** — genuinely different positions, fairly represented,
  each sourced. Not three versions of your own view.
- **Geography** — the physical facts of where the resource is do real
  work in your argument.
- **Care** — Indigenous rights and claims are described accurately, from
  sources connected to those communities.

> [!warning] Three perspectives is a minimum, not a formula
> Industry, government, and "environmentalists" is the lazy set. Workers,
> neighbouring communities, downstream water users, and the people who
> would lose a job if it stopped are all real positions.

See [[Land, Treaty, and Title]] before you write the third bullet, and
[[Arguing About Sustainability]] before you write the fourth.
