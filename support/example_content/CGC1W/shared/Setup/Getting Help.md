Ask early. Nearly everything that becomes a real problem in this course
started as a small question somebody did not ask in week two.

## Ways to reach me

| How | When | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before or after class | Every day | Quick questions, checking your thinking |
| [[Help Sessions]] | The posted times | Working on something with me in the room |
| A booked conversation | Any week | Anything needing more than five minutes |
| Email | Any time; replies on school days | Something you want in writing |

## What to bring

Not "I don't get it" — that is a fine opening, and we get further with
one of these:

- The map or dataset you are stuck on, open.
- Your draft, however rough.
- The question you cannot answer about your inquiry.

## For fieldwork and group work

If your group is not working, come as a group if you can. Most group
problems here are a decision nobody made — about who is recording what,
or what counts as finished.

If a field day is difficult for you for any reason — mobility, health,
transport, anything — tell me in advance and we will arrange it
properly. Everyone gets the fieldwork; how it is done can flex.

## Beyond this course

If something outside class is making school hard, say so, to me or to
guidance. It works better when it starts early, and you do not have to
explain why.
