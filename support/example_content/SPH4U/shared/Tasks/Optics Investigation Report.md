> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Format:** a designed investigation, run and written up. **Pairs.** Due
> at the end of Unit 4. **Assessed on:** Thinking and Investigation.

You design this one. I supply the equipment and the safety veto.

## The brief

Ask a question about the wave behaviour of light that you can answer with
the equipment in this room, then answer it.

Questions that have worked before:

- How does the fringe spacing depend on slit separation, quantitatively?
- What is the thickness of a soap film at the moment a given colour
  vanishes?
- Can a CD be used as a diffraction grating, and what is its track
  spacing?
- How polarized is the light reflected from the lab window, and at what
  angle is it most so?

## Requirements

- A written procedure, checked by me BEFORE anything is switched on.
- A prediction with an uncertainty, submitted before data collection.
- At least one graph whose slope or intercept carries meaning.
- An honest treatment of what limited your precision.
- Prose that meets the standard on [[Writing About Physics]].

## What is not required

A correct answer. A well-designed investigation that produced an
ambiguous result, analysed honestly, scores higher than a copied procedure
that produced the textbook number.
