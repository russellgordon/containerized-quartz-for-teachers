**1.** Find the gravitational force between two $75\ \text{kg}$ students
standing $0.80\ \text{m}$ apart.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> $F = (6.67\times10^{-11})(75)(75)/(0.80)^2 = 5.9 \times 10^{-7}\ \text{N}$
> — about the weight of a grain of dust, which is why nobody notices.

**2.** A satellite orbits at $7.0 \times 10^6\ \text{m}$ from Earth's
centre. Find its speed and period.
($m_E = 5.98 \times 10^{24}\ \text{kg}$)

> [!success]- Answer 2
> $v = \sqrt{Gm_E/r} = 7.5 \times 10^3\ \text{m/s}$;
> $T = 2\pi r/v = 5.8 \times 10^3\ \text{s}$, about 97 minutes.

**3.** Two charges, $+3.0\ \mu\text{C}$ and $-5.0\ \mu\text{C}$, sit
$0.25\ \text{m}$ apart. Find the force between them.

> [!success]- Answer 3
> $F = (8.99\times10^9)(3.0\times10^{-6})(5.0\times10^{-6})/(0.25)^2 = 2.2\ \text{N}$,
> attractive.

**4.** An electron moves at $2.0 \times 10^6\ \text{m/s}$ perpendicular
to a $0.30\ \text{T}$ field. Find the radius of its path.

> [!success]- Answer 4
> $r = mv/(qB) = (9.11\times10^{-31})(2.0\times10^6)/((1.60\times10^{-19})(0.30)) = 3.8 \times 10^{-5}\ \text{m}$.
