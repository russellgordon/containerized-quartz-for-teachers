**1.** A $0.20\ \text{kg}$ ball on a $0.75\ \text{m}$ string swings in
a horizontal circle, completing 20 revolutions in $12\ \text{s}$. Find
the speed and the centripetal force.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> $T = 0.60\ \text{s}$, $v = 2\pi r/T = 7.9\ \text{m/s}$,
> $F_c = mv^2/r = 16.6\ \text{N}$.

**2.** A car of mass $1400\ \text{kg}$ rounds a curve of radius
$55\ \text{m}$ at $18\ \text{m/s}$. What minimum coefficient of static
friction is required?

> [!success]- Answer 2
> $F_c = mv^2/r = 8250\ \text{N}$; $\mu_s \ge F_c/(mg) = 0.60$. Wet
> asphalt is around 0.4, which is why that corner has a warning sign.

**3.** A conical pendulum hangs at $25^\circ$ from the vertical with a
radius of $0.40\ \text{m}$. Find the speed of the bob.

> [!success]- Answer 3
> $v = \sqrt{rg\tan\theta} = \sqrt{0.40 \times 9.8 \times 0.466} = 1.35\ \text{m/s}$.
> Mass never appears — as in [[The Flying Pig]].

**4.** What is the minimum speed at the top of a vertical loop of radius
$8.0\ \text{m}$ for a cart to maintain contact?

> [!success]- Answer 4
> $v = \sqrt{gr} = 8.9\ \text{m/s}$. Below that, the track would need to
> pull the cart inward, which a track cannot do.
