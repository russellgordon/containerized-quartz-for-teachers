**1.** A $0.145\ \text{kg}$ baseball arrives at $38\ \text{m/s}$ and
leaves at $45\ \text{m/s}$ in the opposite direction. Find the impulse
and the average force if contact lasts $0.70\ \text{ms}$.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> $\Delta p = 0.145(45 - (-38)) = 12.0\ \text{kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$;
> $F = \Delta p/\Delta t = 1.7 \times 10^4\ \text{N}$.

**2.** A $2.0\ \text{kg}$ cart at $3.0\ \text{m/s}$ strikes a
stationary $4.0\ \text{kg}$ cart and they stick. Find the final velocity
and the kinetic energy lost.

> [!success]- Answer 2
> $v = (2.0)(3.0)/6.0 = 1.0\ \text{m/s}$. Before:
> $9.0\ \text{J}$; after: $3.0\ \text{J}$. Two thirds lost, to
> deformation, heat, and sound.

**3.** Two skaters, $55\ \text{kg}$ and $70\ \text{kg}$, push apart from
rest. The lighter one moves at $2.8\ \text{m/s}$. Find the other's speed.

> [!success]- Answer 3
> $v = (55 \times 2.8)/70 = 2.2\ \text{m/s}$, opposite direction. Total
> momentum stays zero.

**4.** A $1200\ \text{kg}$ car at $15\ \text{m/s}$ east collides with a
$1800\ \text{kg}$ van at $10\ \text{m/s}$ north and they lock together.
Find the velocity just after.

> [!success]- Answer 4
> $p_x = 18000$, $p_y = 18000$, total mass $3000\ \text{kg}$, so
> $v = \sqrt{2} \times 6.0 = 8.5\ \text{m/s}$ at $45^\circ$ north of
> east.
