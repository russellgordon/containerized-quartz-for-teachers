The point of Unit 3 is not three separate topics. It is that one idea
describes all three, and that the differences are as informative as the
similarities.

| | Gravitational | Electric | Magnetic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source | Mass | Charge | Moving charge |
| Acts on | Mass | Charge | Moving charge |
| Force law | $Gm_1m_2/r^2$ | $kq_1q_2/r^2$ | $qvB\sin\theta$ |
| Direction | Along the line joining | Along the line joining | Perpendicular to both |
| Sign | Attractive only | Both | Both |
| Relative strength | $1$ | $\sim10^{36}$ | Depends on speed |

## The odd one out

Magnetism. Its force is perpendicular rather than radial, it does no work
(the force is always at right angles to the motion), and it requires
motion at both ends. That strangeness is a clue: magnetism is what
electricity looks like from a moving frame — a fact special relativity
explains and Grade 12 only hints at.

## What the table is for

When a new situation arrives, ask which row differs. That is usually where
the physics of the problem lives.
