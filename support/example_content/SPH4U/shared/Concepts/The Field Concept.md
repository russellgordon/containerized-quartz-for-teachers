A field is a way of describing what space does at every point. Instead of
"the Earth pulls the Moon across 380 000 km", we say the Earth changes the
space around it, and the Moon responds to the space where it is.

## Why physics made this move

Because action at a distance is uncomfortable and, once you ask how fast
the pull would update if the Earth moved, unworkable. Fields carry the
influence, at a finite speed, and they hold energy of their own.

## The same shape three times

| Field | Source | Test object | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gravitational | Mass | Small mass | $g = F_g/m$ |
| Electric | Charge | Small positive charge | $\varepsilon = F_e/q$ |
| Magnetic | Moving charge | Moving charge | $B = F_m/(qv)$ |

Each is force per unit of something, each falls off with distance, and
each is drawn with lines whose density means strength. Noticing that they
are the same idea three times is the point of this unit — see
[[Comparing the Three Fields]].
