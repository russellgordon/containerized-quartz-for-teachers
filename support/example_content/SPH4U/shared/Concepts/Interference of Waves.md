When two waves overlap, their displacements add. Where they arrive in
step, they reinforce; where they arrive half a cycle apart, they cancel.

## Path difference decides everything

For two sources in phase:

- **Constructive** where the path difference is a whole number of
  wavelengths: $\Delta d = n\lambda$
- **Destructive** where it is a half-integer number:
  $\Delta d = (n + \tfrac{1}{2})\lambda$

The pattern of nodal and antinodal lines that results is the same in a
ripple tank, in sound from two speakers, and in light from two slits. The
physics does not care what is waving.

## Seeing it in water first

A ripple tank makes the pattern visible and slow enough to think about,
which is why [[Ripple Tank Interference]] comes before the laser. Students
who have watched nodal lines sweep across water are never mystified by
dark fringes on a screen.
