$$F_e = \frac{kq_1q_2}{r^2}$$

The force between two point charges: proportional to both charges, falling
off as the square of the separation. The identical shape to gravitation is
not a coincidence anyone has fully explained, and physicists still find it
interesting.

## The differences that matter

| | Gravity | Electric |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign | Always attractive | Attracts or repels |
| Strength | Feeble | Enormous |
| Shielding | Impossible | Routine |

"Enormous" deserves a number: between two protons, the electric repulsion
is about $10^{36}$ times the gravitational attraction. Gravity only wins
at astronomical scales because matter is electrically neutral on average.

## Superposition

With more than two charges, compute each pairwise force and add them as
vectors. There is no shortcut, and the vector addition is where the marks
live — see [[Working with Vectors]].
