The pages in this folder are the Ministry of Education's expectations for
SPH4U, reproduced word for word. Nothing here is a paraphrase: when a
lesson or task says it addresses B2.6, you can follow the link and
read exactly what the province wrote.

## Where the wording comes from

> [!quote] Citation
> *The Ontario Curriculum, Grades 11 and 12: Science, 2008 (Revised)*.
> Toronto: Ontario Ministry of Education, 2008.
> Physics, Grade 12, University Preparation (SPH4U), pages 194–207.
> © Queen's Printer for Ontario, 2008. Reproduced for classroom use.

- The document itself:
  [2009science11_12.pdf](https://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/curriculum/secondary/2009science11_12.pdf)
- The course on the Ministry's curriculum site:
  [SPH4U at dcp.edu.gov.on.ca](https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/secondary-science/courses/sph4u-pdf)

The 2008 science curriculum is still the one in force for Grades 11 and
12, which is why a document with that date is the current source.

## What the codes mean

Every expectation has a code, and the code says where it sits:

| Part | Means |
| --- | --- |
| The letter | The strand — A skills, B dynamics, C energy and momentum, D fields, E the wave nature of light, F modern physics |
| The first number | Which of the strand's three overall expectations |
| The number after the dot | Which specific expectation within it |

So B2.6 is the sixth specific expectation under overall
expectation B2 in strand B.

The three overall expectations in each content strand always ask the same
three things: **1** relates the science to technology, society, and the
environment; **2** develops the skills of investigation and communication;
**3** builds understanding of the basic concepts. A course that only did
the third one would not be this course.

## The square brackets

Most specific expectations end with something like `[IP, PR, AI, C]`.
Those name the investigation skills the expectation draws on —
**I**nitiating and **P**lanning, **P**erforming and **R**ecording,
**A**nalysing and **I**nterpreting, and **C**ommunicating. They are the
Ministry's, not ours, and they are part of the wording.

## What is not here

The Ministry prints *sample questions* and *sample issues* beside many
expectations. They are prompts for teachers, not requirements, so they are
not reproduced on these pages — the expectation itself is what a lesson
has to meet.

> [!note] One expectation is reproduced with a printing error intact
> B3.3 reads "the variables frequency, period, radius speed, and mass" in
> the published document — a comma is missing between *radius* and
> *speed*. It is quoted here exactly as printed, because a curriculum page
> that silently corrects its source stops being a quotation.
