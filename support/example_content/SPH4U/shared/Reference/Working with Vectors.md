Grade 11 let you get away with signs. Grade 12 does not: almost everything
in Units 1 and 3 is a vector in a plane.

## Components

Any vector splits into perpendicular parts:

$$A_x = A\cos\theta \qquad A_y = A\sin\theta$$

and rebuilds from them:

$$A = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2} \qquad \theta = \tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{A_y}{A_x}\right)$$

The angle is measured from the axis you say it is measured from. Say it.

## Adding

Add components, never magnitudes. $3\ \text{N}$ east plus
$4\ \text{N}$ north is $5\ \text{N}$, not $7\ \text{N}$ — and the
direction is $53^\circ$ north of east, which is part of the answer.

## Choosing axes

You may point the axes anywhere, and choosing well is most of the work:

- **Inclines:** along and perpendicular to the slope.
- **Projectiles:** horizontal and vertical, because they are independent.
- **Circular motion:** toward the centre and tangent to the circle.

## The check that catches sign errors

Sketch the resultant before computing it. If your arithmetic gives a
direction the sketch does not, believe the sketch and find the error.
