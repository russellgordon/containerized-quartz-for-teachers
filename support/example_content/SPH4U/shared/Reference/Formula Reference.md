Everything this course uses. Choosing correctly is the skill; this list
exists so you are not hunting.

## Motion and dynamics

$$\vec{F}_{net} = m\vec{a} \qquad F_f = \mu F_N \qquad F_g = mg$$

$$a_c = \frac{v^2}{r} = \frac{4\pi^2 r}{T^2} \qquad F_c = \frac{mv^2}{r}$$

## Energy and momentum

$$W = Fd\cos\theta \qquad E_k = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2 \qquad E_g = mg\Delta h$$

$$E_e = \tfrac{1}{2}kx^2 \qquad F_s = -kx \qquad T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}$$

$$\vec{p} = m\vec{v} \qquad \vec{J} = \vec{F}\Delta t = \Delta \vec{p}$$

## Fields

$$F_g = \frac{Gm_1m_2}{r^2} \qquad g = \frac{Gm}{r^2}$$

$$F_e = \frac{kq_1q_2}{r^2} \qquad \varepsilon = \frac{F_e}{q} \qquad V = \frac{kq}{r}$$

$$F_m = qvB\sin\theta \qquad F = BIL\sin\theta$$

## Waves and light

$$v = f\lambda \qquad \frac{\sin\theta_1}{\sin\theta_2} = \frac{v_1}{v_2} = n \qquad \lambda = \frac{d\sin\theta}{n}$$

## Modern physics

$$E = hf \qquad E_k = hf - W \qquad \lambda = \frac{h}{p}$$

$$\Delta t = \frac{\Delta t_s}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}} \qquad L = L_s\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2} \qquad E = mc^2$$
