$$E_g = mg\Delta h$$

The energy stored by lifting something, in joules. It is potential in the
plain sense: it will become motion if you let go.

## Height from where?

From wherever you choose — and the choice cannot change any answer,
because only the CHANGE in height appears in the equation. Pick the lowest
point of the problem as your zero, say so, and move on. Arguments about
"true" height are arguments about nothing.

## It is stored in the field, not the object

Strictly, gravitational potential energy belongs to the object-and-Earth
system, not to the object. This matters not at all for calculations and
quite a lot for the sentence "the ball has potential energy" — which is
shorthand, and worth knowing as shorthand.

## The exchange

Drop the object and $E_g$ becomes [[Kinetic Energy]] at a rate the
equations of [[Conservation of Energy]] make exact. That exchange, run
back and forth along a track, is a roller coaster — see
[[Model Roller Coaster]].
