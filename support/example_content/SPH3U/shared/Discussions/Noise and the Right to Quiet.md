Noise is sound somebody did not consent to. Where is the line, and who
gets to draw it?

## The physics you need first

Loudness is measured on a logarithmic scale: every 10 dB is a factor of
ten in intensity, and roughly a doubling in perceived loudness. A busy
road at 80 dB is not "a bit louder" than a conversation at 60 dB — it
carries a hundred times the intensity.

Hearing damage depends on both level and duration. 85 dB for eight hours,
100 dB for fifteen minutes, and 110 dB for under two minutes carry
comparable risk.

## The question

Your school is beside a road, a rail line, or a flight path (pick the one
that is true here). Whose responsibility is the noise: the source, the
building, or the person who chose to be there?

## Take it further

Bring the decibel reading from your own phone, in a place you care about,
with the time of day. A measurement changes a discussion.
