Every equation this course uses, in one place. Knowing which one to reach
for is the skill; the list is just so you do not have to hunt.

## Motion

$$v_{av} = \frac{\Delta d}{\Delta t} \qquad a_{av} = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}$$

$$\Delta d = \left(\frac{v_1 + v_2}{2}\right)\Delta t \qquad \Delta d = v_1\Delta t + \tfrac{1}{2}a(\Delta t)^2 \qquad v_2^2 = v_1^2 + 2a\Delta d$$

## Forces

$$F_{net} = ma \qquad F_g = mg \qquad F_f = \mu F_N$$

## Energy

$$W = Fd\cos\theta \qquad E_k = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2 \qquad E_g = mg\Delta h$$

$$P = \frac{W}{\Delta t} \qquad \text{efficiency} = \frac{E_{out}}{E_{in}} \times 100\%$$

$$Q = mc\Delta T \qquad Q = mL_\text{f} \qquad Q = mL_\text{v}$$

## Waves and sound

$$v = f\lambda \qquad T = \frac{1}{f} \qquad v_{sound} = 331 + 0.6T$$

## Electricity

$$V = IR \qquad P = IV \qquad R_{series} = R_1 + R_2 \qquad \frac{1}{R_{parallel}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}$$
