Your first [[Physics Journal]] entry is written in class, so the format is
never a mystery afterwards.

## The prompt

After [[Walking a Graph]], write three quarters of a page:

1. **In your own words:** what does the slope of a position–time graph
   tell you? Do not use the word "slope" more than once.
2. **An example:** sketch the graph your group produced for one of the
   four targets, and say in two sentences what a person watching would
   have seen.
3. **Still murky:** one question you would ask if there were no cost to
   asking it.

## What a good third question looks like

- Weak: "I do not understand graphs."
- Better: "I do not see how a curve can have a slope at a single point."
- Best: "If the tangent gives the velocity at an instant, what is the
  velocity at the exact moment the walker turns around?"

The third one is a genuinely good physics question, and a student who
writes it has understood more than they think.
