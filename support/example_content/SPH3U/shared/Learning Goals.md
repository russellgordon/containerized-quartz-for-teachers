By the end of this course you will be able to look at something moving and
say what is making it move that way — and back it with a measurement.

In plain words, you will be able to:

- describe motion precisely enough that someone else could reproduce it,
  in words, in graphs, and in equations;
- draw the forces on an object and predict what it will do next;
- follow energy through a system and say where it went;
- explain sound as a wave, and predict what a wave will do when it meets
  something;
- build and analyse a circuit, and explain the link between electricity
  and magnetism.
