## Agenda

1. Number talk: [[True or False]] — a car's average speed over an hour
   can be zero while the car is never at rest
2. Random groups: build one formula from two ideas — a secant's slope,
   then the limit that shrinks it onto the point
3. Consolidate: [[The Derivative]] — the definition your boards just
   assembled, made official
4. Launch: [[The Speed Camera]] — one ticket, one verdict, every rate
   defended
5. How it is assessed: [[How Marks Work]] — reasoning is the product,
   and [[Writing About Math]] is how you hand it over
