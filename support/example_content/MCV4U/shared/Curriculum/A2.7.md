recognize that the natural logarithmic function f(x) = log_e x, also written as f(x) = ln x, is the inverse of the exponential function f(x) = e^x, and make connections between f(x) = ln x and f(x) = e^x [e.g., f(x) = ln x reverses what f(x) = e^x does; their graphs are reflections of each other in the line y = x; the composition of the two functions, e^(ln x) or ln e^x, maps x onto itself, that is, e^(ln x) = x and ln e^x = x] ^text
