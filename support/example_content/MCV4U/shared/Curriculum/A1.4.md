recognize, through investigation with or without technology, graphical and numerical examples of limits, and explain the reasoning involved (e.g., the value of a function approaching an asymptote, the value of the ratio of successive terms in the Fibonacci sequence)
*Sample problem:* Use appropriate technology to investigate the limiting value of the terms in the sequence $\left(1 + \frac{1}{1}\right)^1$, $\left(1 + \frac{1}{2}\right)^2$, $\left(1 + \frac{1}{3}\right)^3$, $\left(1 + \frac{1}{4}\right)^4$, …, and the limiting value of the series $4 \times 1 - 4 \times \frac{1}{3} + 4 \times \frac{1}{5} - 4 \times \frac{1}{7} + 4 \times \frac{1}{9} - \ldots$. ^text
