In order of speed.

1. **Ask the person beside you.** Talking about a text with somebody is
   the method, not a shortcut around it.
2. **Read the page.** [[Close Reading]], [[The Essay]],
   [[Citing Sources]], and [[Getting Unstuck]] answer most of what gets
   asked.
3. **Ask me during the work part of the period.** That is why I am
   walking the room.
4. **Come to [[Help Sessions]]** — Mondays and Wednesdays at lunch.
5. **Email me.** Slowest, still works.

## Four sentences that get you moving

- "I do not know what the question is asking."
- "I have a claim and I cannot find evidence for it."
- "I have evidence and I cannot say what it proves."
- "I read the scene twice and I have nothing."

The last one is the most common with Shakespeare, and the fix is
mechanical: we read twenty lines aloud together and paraphrase them line
by line. It takes six minutes and it works every time.

> [!tip] Say it early
> "I am behind" in week two is a scheduling conversation. In week
> nineteen it is a crisis. Nobody has ever been told off in this room for
> saying it early.
