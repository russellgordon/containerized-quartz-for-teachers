The method is on [[Reading Shakespeare]]: unscramble the syntax, check
the false friends, read to the punctuation.

**1.** *"But soft, what light through yonder window breaks? / It is the
east, and Juliet is the sun."*

> [!success]- Answer 1
> Wait — what is that light coming through that window over there? It is
> the east, and Juliet is the sunrise.
> Note what a paraphrase loses: "breaks" (violent, sudden) becomes
> "coming through". That loss is exactly why we analyse the original
> rather than the paraphrase.

**2.** *"Deny thy father and refuse thy name; / Or, if thou wilt not, be
but sworn my love, / And I'll no longer be a Capulet."*

> [!success]- Answer 2
> Disown your father and give up your name. Or if you will not do that,
> just swear you love me, and I will stop being a Capulet.
> Notice the order: she asks him first, and offers herself second. That
> sequence is worth a paragraph.

**3.** *"A plague o' both your houses! / They have made worms' meat of
me."*

> [!success]- Answer 3
> Curse both your families. They have turned me into food for worms —
> that is, they have killed me.
> "Worms' meat" is the point: Mercutio refuses a dignified death speech
> and describes his body as material. The joke is still a joke, and it is
> also his last one.

**4.** *"These violent delights have violent ends, / And in their triumph
die, like fire and powder, / Which as they kiss consume."*

> [!success]- Answer 4
> Passions this intense end violently; at their high point they destroy
> themselves, the way fire and gunpowder destroy each other the moment
> they touch.
> The Friar says this at the wedding. Ask why Shakespeare puts the play's
> clearest warning in the mouth of the man who marries them.

**5.** Take twenty lines from the scene your group is staging and
paraphrase them in the right-hand column of your notebook. Mark the two
lines you could not do.

> [!success]- Answer 5
> No fixed answer — the marks are the point. Bring them to class; twenty
> lines paraphrased with two marks is a better use of a period than forty
> lines skimmed.
