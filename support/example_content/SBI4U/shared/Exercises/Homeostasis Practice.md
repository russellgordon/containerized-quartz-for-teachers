**1.** Identify the receptor, control centre, and effector for
thermoregulation when you are too warm.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> Receptors: thermoreceptors in skin and hypothalamus. Control centre:
> hypothalamus. Effectors: skin arterioles (vasodilation), sweat glands,
> and behaviour.

**2.** Explain why an action potential is all-or-none, and how intensity
is encoded instead.

> [!success]- Answer 2
> Once threshold is passed, the sodium channels open fully and the spike
> is the same size regardless of stimulus. Intensity is encoded as
> frequency — more spikes per second.

**3.** Type 1 and type 2 diabetes both raise blood glucose. Where does the
loop fail in each?

> [!success]- Answer 3
> Type 1: the effector's output is gone — beta cells destroyed, so no
> insulin. Type 2: the target cells stop responding to the signal —
> insulin resistance.

**4.** Why is filter-then-reabsorb a sensible kidney design?

> [!success]- Answer 4
> A size-based filter is fast and non-specific, and selectivity is applied
> afterwards by transporters. Being selective at the filter would need a
> mechanism for every unwanted molecule, including novel ones.

**5.** Give one example of positive feedback in the body, and say what
ends it.

> [!success]- Answer 5
> Childbirth: oxytocin increases contractions, which increase oxytocin.
> Delivery ends it. Blood clotting is another, ended by the clot.
