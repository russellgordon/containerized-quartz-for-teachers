**1.** A template strand reads 3′-TACGGCTTAACT-5′. Give the mRNA and the
peptide.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> mRNA: 5′-AUGCCGAAUUGA-3′. Codons AUG CCG AAU UGA →
> methionine–proline–asparagine–stop.

**2.** Why must the lagging strand be built in fragments?

> [!success]- Answer 2
> Polymerase only synthesises 5′ to 3′, and the templates are
> antiparallel. The lagging template forces discontinuous synthesis in
> Okazaki fragments, joined later by ligase.

**3.** Distinguish a silent, a missense, and a nonsense mutation by
effect.

> [!success]- Answer 3
> Silent: no change to the amino acid, thanks to code redundancy.
> Missense: one amino acid substituted. Nonsense: a premature stop codon,
> truncating the protein.

**4.** In the lac operon, what happens when lactose is present?

> [!success]- Answer 4
> Lactose binds the repressor, which then cannot bind the operator, so RNA
> polymerase transcribes the genes for lactose metabolism.

**5.** Two identical twins differ in a trait. Give a molecular explanation
that does not involve a difference in DNA sequence.

> [!success]- Answer 5
> Epigenetic differences — methylation or histone modification — altering
> which genes are expressed. See [[Regulating Gene Expression]].
