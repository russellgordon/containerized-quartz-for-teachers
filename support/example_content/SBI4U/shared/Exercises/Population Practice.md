**1.** A population of 200 grows at $r = 0.15$ per year with unlimited
resources. Find its size after 3 years.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> $200 \times 1.15^3 = 304$ individuals.

**2.** With $K = 1000$ and $N = 900$, what fraction of the maximum growth
rate remains?

> [!success]- Answer 2
> $(K - N)/K = 0.1$ — one tenth. Growth is nearly stalled even though the
> population is still below carrying capacity.

**3.** Mark and recapture: 60 marked, later 80 caught of which 12 are
marked. Estimate the population.

> [!success]- Answer 3
> $N = (60 \times 80)/12 = 400$.

**4.** Which assumption of that method is most likely violated if marking
makes an animal more visible to predators, and which way is your estimate
wrong?

> [!success]- Answer 4
> That marking does not affect survival. Fewer marked animals survive to
> be recaptured, so $R$ is too small and the estimate is too HIGH.

**5.** Distinguish density-dependent from density-independent limiting
factors, with an example of each.

> [!success]- Answer 5
> Density-dependent intensifies as the population grows: disease,
> competition, predation. Density-independent does not: a hard frost, a
> flood, a fire.
