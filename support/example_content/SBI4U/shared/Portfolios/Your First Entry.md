Your first [[Biology Journal]] entry is written in class, so the format is
never a mystery afterwards.

## The prompt

After [[Enzyme Activity]], write three quarters of a page:

1. **In your own words:** why does an enzyme have an optimum temperature?
   Do not use the word "denature" until you have explained what happens.
2. **A graph:** your temperature curve, hand-drawn, with the peak marked
   and one sentence on what happens either side of it.
3. **Still murky:** one question you would ask if there were no cost to
   asking it.

## What a good third question looks like

- Weak: "I do not understand enzymes."
- Better: "Why does the rate fall so sharply after the optimum rather than
  gradually?"
- Best: "If denaturation is irreversible, why does the rate not go to
  zero the moment we pass the optimum?"

The last one has a real answer involving the distribution of molecular
energies, and a student who asks it is thinking like a biochemist.
