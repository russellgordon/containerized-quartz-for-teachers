By the end of this course you will be able to explain how a living thing
works at the level of molecules, and connect that explanation upward to
cells, organisms, and populations.

In plain words, you will be able to:

- explain what biological molecules do, and why their structure decides
  it;
- trace energy through respiration and photosynthesis, step by step;
- explain how DNA is expressed, regulated, and manipulated;
- explain how a body holds conditions steady, and what happens when a
  control system fails;
- model how populations grow and what limits them.
