A mutation is a change in the DNA sequence. Most do nothing; some are
disastrous; a few are the raw material of everything.

## Point mutations

- **Silent:** the codon changes, the amino acid does not (thanks to the
  code's redundancy).
- **Missense:** one amino acid is substituted. Sickle-cell disease is one
  such change.
- **Nonsense:** a stop codon appears early, truncating the protein.

## Frameshift

An insertion or deletion of one or two bases shifts every codon after it.
The protein downstream is nonsense. This is why the deletion in
[[Modelling Molecular Genetics]] does so much more damage than the
substitution.

## Where they come from

Replication errors, radiation (UV, X-rays), and chemical mutagens.
Germline mutations are inherited; somatic ones are not, but can cause
cancer in the individual.

## Why the rate is not zero

A mutation rate of zero means no variation, and no variation means no
adaptation. Every repair system is a compromise between fidelity and the
cost of achieving it.
