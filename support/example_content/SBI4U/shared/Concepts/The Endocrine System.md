Slow control, by hormones in the blood. Where the nervous system sends a
targeted message in milliseconds, the endocrine system broadcasts and only
cells with the right receptor respond.

| Gland | Hormone | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pancreas | Insulin, glucagon | Lower, raise blood glucose |
| Thyroid | Thyroxine | Metabolic rate |
| Adrenal medulla | Adrenaline | Fight or flight |
| Adrenal cortex | Cortisol | Longer-term stress response |
| Pituitary | ADH, and many others | Water retention; controls other glands |

## Two kinds of hormone

**Steroid** hormones are lipid-soluble, pass through the membrane, and act
on genes directly — slow, lasting. **Protein** hormones bind surface
receptors and trigger second messengers — faster, briefer. The chemistry
from [[Carbohydrates and Lipids]] decides which route a hormone takes.

## Feedback again

The hypothalamus–pituitary–thyroid axis is a negative feedback loop with
three levels. Rising thyroxine suppresses the signals that produced it,
which is exactly the structure on
[[Homeostasis and Feedback]] with more steps.
