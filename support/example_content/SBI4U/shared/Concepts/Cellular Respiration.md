$$\ce{C6H12O6 + 6O2 -> 6CO2 + 6H2O}$$

Glucose is oxidised in controlled steps, and the energy released is
captured as ATP rather than heat. The control is the entire point: burning
glucose in a flame releases the same energy and captures none of it.

## Four stages

| Stage | Where | Yields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glycolysis | Cytoplasm | 2 ATP, 2 NADH, 2 pyruvate |
| Pyruvate oxidation | Mitochondrial matrix | 2 NADH, 2 acetyl-CoA, 2 $\ce{CO2}$ |
| Krebs cycle | Matrix | 2 ATP, 6 NADH, 2 $\ce{FADH2}$, 4 $\ce{CO2}$ |
| Electron transport and chemiosmosis | Inner membrane | About 26–28 ATP |

## Chemiosmosis is the mechanism worth understanding

Electrons passed down the transport chain power proton pumping into the
intermembrane space. The protons flow back through ATP synthase, and that
flow turns a molecular rotor that makes ATP.

It is a waterwheel, and calling it one is not a simplification — Peter
Mitchell won a Nobel Prize for the proposal, which was rejected for years
because it did not look like chemistry.

## Without oxygen

Fermentation regenerates NAD⁺ so glycolysis can continue: lactic acid in
animals, ethanol and $\ce{CO2}$ in yeast. The yield collapses to 2 ATP per
glucose, which is why sprinting is short.
