$$\ce{6CO2 + 6H2O ->[light] C6H12O6 + 6O2}$$

Grade 11 gave the equation. This year gives the mechanism, and the
mechanism turns out to be respiration's mirror image in several respects.

## Light-dependent reactions

In the thylakoid membrane. Photosystems II and I absorb light; water is
split (releasing the oxygen); electrons travel a transport chain; protons
are pumped into the thylakoid space; ATP synthase makes ATP as they flow
back. NADPH is produced at the end.

Chemiosmosis again — the same machinery as
[[Cellular Respiration]], in a different membrane, for a different
purpose.

## The Calvin cycle

In the stroma. Carbon dioxide is fixed onto RuBP by rubisco, the most
abundant protein on Earth. ATP and NADPH from the light reactions reduce
the product to G3P, and most G3P is recycled to regenerate RuBP.

Three turns of the cycle fix three carbons; six turns yield one glucose.

## Rubisco's flaw

It also binds oxygen, wastefully — photorespiration. $\ce{C4}$ and CAM
plants have evolved workarounds that concentrate carbon dioxide around the
enzyme, which is why maize and cacti outperform wheat in heat.
