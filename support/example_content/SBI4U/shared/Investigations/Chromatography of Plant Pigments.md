> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Time:** half a class. **Pairs.** Calculate an Rf for every band.

Leaf pigments separate on paper because they differ in solubility and in
how strongly they cling to the paper.

## Safety

The solvent is volatile and flammable: fume hood or well-ventilated bench,
no flames, lid on the chamber.

## Method

1. Grind leaf tissue and spot the extract on the pencil origin line.
2. Let the solvent rise until it nears the top; mark the front
   immediately.
3. Measure each band's distance and the solvent front's distance.

$$R_f = \frac{\text{distance moved by the pigment}}{\text{distance moved by the solvent}}$$

Record the plate with a labelled sketch — the conventions are on
[[Biological Drawing]].

## Analysis

1. Identify the pigments by colour and Rf: chlorophyll a, chlorophyll b,
   xanthophylls, carotenes.
2. Why do the carotenes travel furthest? Answer in terms of polarity.
3. Green leaves contain all four. What does that predict about autumn
   colour, and does it match what you see?
4. Connect the pigment range to the absorption spectrum and to the light
   reactions of [[Photosynthesis in Detail]].
