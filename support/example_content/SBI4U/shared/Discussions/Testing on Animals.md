Almost every drug you have taken was tested on animals first, and most
biology we teach was learned that way.

## The biology you need first

Why model organisms are used at all: shared physiology, shared genetics,
and the fact that a feedback loop can only be fully tested in a whole
organism — see [[Homeostasis and Feedback]].

## The framework in use

The "three Rs" — replacement, reduction, refinement — are the standard in
Canadian research, overseen by animal care committees. They are a
harm-minimisation framework, not a claim that the harm is zero.

## The question

Where does your line sit: species, purpose, or degree of suffering? And
does a treatment developed through animal testing carry an obligation on
the person who benefits from it?

Our own [[Working with Living Things]] page is this argument applied to a
classroom, at much lower stakes.
