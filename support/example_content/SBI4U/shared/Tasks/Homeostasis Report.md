> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Format:** four pages with original diagrams. **Individually.**
> **Due:** end of Unit 4. **Assessed on:** Knowledge, Thinking,
> Communication.

Take a disorder in which a feedback loop fails, and explain the failure at
every level: molecule, cell, system, person.

## Choose one

Type 1 or type 2 diabetes · hyperthyroidism or hypothyroidism · diabetes
insipidus · Addison's disease · multiple sclerosis · chronic kidney
disease.

## What to produce

1. **The intact loop**, diagrammed: stimulus, receptor, control centre,
   effector, response.
2. **The break**, located precisely — which component fails, and how.
3. **The consequences**, followed through at least two systems.
4. **Treatment**, and which part of the loop it substitutes for.
5. **What it cannot fix**, and why.

Point 5 is where the strongest reports separate themselves: insulin
replaces the effector's output but not its regulation, and saying exactly
that is a Grade 12 answer.
