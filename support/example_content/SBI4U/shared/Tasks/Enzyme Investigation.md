You design this one. I supply the enzymes and the veto.

## The brief

Ask a question about enzyme behaviour that the equipment here can answer,
then answer it. Your procedure is approved before anything is opened.

Questions that have worked: does the optimum temperature of catalase
differ between liver and potato? Does an inhibitor's effect depend on
substrate concentration? Does pepsin's optimum pH match the stomach's?

## Requirements

- One manipulated variable, at least five levels, three trials each.
- Controlled variables listed and actually controlled.
- A graph whose shape you explain with protein structure.
- An uncertainty treatment — biological material varies, and saying by how
  much is part of the science.

The report structure is on [[Writing a Lab Report]], and the sentence-level
habits on [[Writing About Biology]].

## What separates a strong report

Explaining the SHAPE of the curve, not just its peak. A rate that falls
after an optimum and a rate that plateaus are two different mechanisms,
and a strong report names which is which and why.
