Grade 12 biology is where the mechanisms arrive. In Grade 11 you learned
that photosynthesis makes sugar; this year you find out how, at the level
of electrons and enzymes, and why that level is the one that explains
everything else.

## The shape of a class

1. **A problem, usually molecular.** Data from a real experiment, a
   pathway with a step missing, a patient's numbers.
2. **Work it in groups.** These pathways are too intricate to absorb by
   watching someone else trace them.
3. **Name it and connect it.** Every mechanism this year connects
   upward — to the cell, the organism, the population.
4. **Practise.** See [[Exercises/index|Exercises]].

## What this course assumes

Cell structure, DNA and protein basics, meiosis, and the idea of
homeostasis. If any of that is shaky, say so in the first week; it is the
floor everything else stands on.

## The lab

More independent design than Grade 11. Several investigations give you a
question and a bench — [[Safety in the Lab]] and
[[Working with Living Things]] apply to all of them, and your procedure
is checked before anything is switched on.

## Getting unstuck

Bring the diagram. A pathway you have drawn badly is a better starting
point than a question you have phrased well. See [[Getting Help]] and
[[Help Sessions]].
