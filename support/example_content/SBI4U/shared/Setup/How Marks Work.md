## What is assessed

Everything I mark has its own page in [[Tasks/index|Tasks]], and every one
of those pages says how it will be judged before you start it. If a task
page does not say, that is a bug — tell me and I will fix the page.

## What is not assessed

Practice sets. Rough work. The first version of a lab write-up. Being
wrong in a discussion. If it is not on a task page, it is not being
marked, and you should take more risks with it than you probably will.

## The four categories

Ontario reports physics in four categories, and they measure genuinely
different things:

| Category | The question it asks |
| --- | --- |
| Knowledge and Understanding | Do you know the physics, and can you explain it? |
| Thinking and Investigation | Can you plan an investigation and reason from data? |
| Communication | Can another person follow your reasoning? |
| Application | Can you use the physics somewhere it has not been used for you? |

A perfect calculation with no diagram, no units, and no sentence is not a
perfect answer. It is a strong Knowledge answer and a weak Communication
one, and the report card will say so.

## Late work

Talk to me before the due date and it is a conversation. Talk to me after
and it is a mark. That is the whole policy.
