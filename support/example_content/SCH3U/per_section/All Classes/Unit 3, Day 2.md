Last class ended unfinished. Today you build the bridge from mass to
count with rice, then walk back across it and answer the paperclip.

## Agenda

1. Count rice by weighing — twenty grains first, then a full beaker
2. Skill as you go: [[Measuring Well]] — tare, resolution, repeated readings
3. What your rice method needs before it works for atoms
4. Name it: [[The Mole]] — Avogadro's number, against your rice constant
5. Return to the paperclip and finish it, with units and significant figures

## Things to do before our next class

- [ ] Journal: your rice "mole", how many grains it holds, and why your
      number differs from the group beside you.
- [ ] Re-read [[The Mole]] and bring one question about the worked example.
