You can convert for elements now. Compounds are the same move made
twice, and today is where it stops needing thought.

## Agenda

1. Weigh out what you believe is one mole of three different substances
2. Check each other's masses against the periodic table — who was closest?
3. Name it: [[Molar Mass and Composition]] — compounds, and percent by mass
4. Where percentage composition already tells you something worth knowing
5. Practise: [[Mole Conversions Practice]] in pairs, one checking the other
