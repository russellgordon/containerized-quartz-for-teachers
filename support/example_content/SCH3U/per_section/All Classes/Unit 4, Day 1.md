Eight solids, three solvents, and a written prediction from each of you
before anything goes anywhere near the water.

## Agenda

1. Predict which of the eight will dissolve, and commit it to paper
2. Test all eight in water, then in the two other solvents provided
3. Sort your results — what do the dissolvers have in common?
4. Name it: [[Water and Solutions]] — polarity, read against your sorting
5. Check your grid against [[Solubility Rules]] — argue with the exceptions

## Things to do before our next class

- [ ] Journal: the prediction you got most wrong, and the reason you can
      now give for it.
- [ ] Read [[Water and Solutions]] and mark the paragraph that explains
      your own grid best.
