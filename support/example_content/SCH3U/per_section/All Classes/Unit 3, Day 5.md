You are told how much product is wanted and asked how much to start
with. You can already do every piece of that.

## Agenda

1. Given a target mass of product, work backwards to the reactant mass
2. Run it — weigh out your own calculated amount and see what you get
3. Compare predicted against actual across the room, significant figures kept
4. Name it: [[Stoichiometry]] — the mole ratio as the step in the middle
5. Practise: [[Stoichiometry Practice]], in pairs, showing every step
