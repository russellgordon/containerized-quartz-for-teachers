## Extra help

| When | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Monday, lunch | The chemistry room |
| Wednesday, after school until 4:15 | The chemistry room |

Drop in — no appointment, no need to tell me first. Bring the specific
thing you are stuck on: one stoichiometry question that will not come
out beats "the mole". Lab write-ups count too, and so does a result that
did not do what you expected — that is usually the most interesting
conversation of the week.

Nothing gets set up, heated, or poured at lunch without me in the room.
