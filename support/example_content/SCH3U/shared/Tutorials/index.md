How to do the things this course keeps asking of you — the label, the
glassware, the report, the data table, the digits at the end of a
calculation.

Come back to these. Nobody remembers on first hearing why the rule for
adding measurements differs from the rule for multiplying them, and that
is exactly why it is written down.

Two are worth reading before your first lab day:
[[Lab Safety and WHMIS]] and [[Measuring Well]]. The one students
return to most across the semester is
[[Significant Figures in Practice]].
