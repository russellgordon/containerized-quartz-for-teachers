Your own record of how your thinking changed over the semester.

A journal is **chosen, not accumulated**. A handful of entries written
close to the work, while you still remember which reading you doubted
and why, beats a folder containing everything you ever handed in.

Start with [[Chemistry Journal]] for what this is and why we bother,
then write [[Your First Entry]] in the first week.
[[What a Strong Entry Looks Like]] shows you the standard, and
[[Journal Checklist]] is what I check against at the end.
