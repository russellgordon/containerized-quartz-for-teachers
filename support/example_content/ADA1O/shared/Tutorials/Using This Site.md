Everything for this course lives on this site, and it is built to be used in
a particular way. Five minutes here saves you an hour of wandering later.

## Start where the site starts

The landing page for your section always shows the **most recent class** —
agenda, what we did, what is due. If you were away, that page is your first
stop; if you were here, it is still the quickest answer to "what was that
thing she said about Thursday?" Older class pages sit right behind it, one
per day, so catching up after a week away is a matter of reading five short
pages in order.

## Three habits that make the site fast

1. **Search first.** Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or
   <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>) and type any word — a convention, a task
   name, a phrase you half remember. Search is almost always faster than
   browsing folders.
2. **Hover before you click.** Rest your pointer on any link — try
   [[Tableau]] — and the page appears in a small preview window. You can
   check a term mid-sentence without losing your place.
3. **Read the backlinks.** At the bottom of every page is a list of every
   page that links *to* it. On [[Hot Seating]], the backlinks show you each
   class and task where we used it — a ready-made review list nobody had to
   build.

## Where things live

The short version: class pages record what happened, [[Concepts/index|Concepts]]
explain ideas, [[Conventions/index|Conventions]] are tools, Tasks are the
assignments, and your [[Drama Journal]] instructions live in Portfolios. The
full map, folder by folder, is [[How This Site Is Organised]] — and if you
are curious why the pages can do what they do (previews, diagrams, embedded
curriculum), that tour is [[What This Site Can Do]].

> [!tip] The habit that gets you unstuck
> Lost in a class page? Follow its link to the concept. Lost in a concept?
> Use its backlinks to find the class where we did it. The two directions,
> used together, mean you are never more than two clicks from your footing.
