A tutorial is a **how-to**: the steps for something this course asks you to
do more than once. The [[Concepts/index|Concepts]] pages explain ideas and
the [[Conventions/index|Conventions]] pages hand you tools, but when the
question is *how do I actually do this* — get lines into memory, run a
useful rehearsal, give a note that helps — the answer lives here.

Come back to these. Nobody remembers how to take a note gracefully the
first time someone gives them one.

> [!tip] Read them when you need them
> None of these is homework. Each one exists for a moment that will arrive
> on its own: the week before [[Tableau Story Sequence]] is performed, the
> day you first hear "let's work that scene," the night you realise your
> lines are not as learned as you thought. When the moment comes, the page
> will be here.
