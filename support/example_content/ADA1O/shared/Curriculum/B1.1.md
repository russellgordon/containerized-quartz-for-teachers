> [!question]- For teachers: the Ministry's prompts for B1.1
> "What roles do you need in your group to produce this piece of work? What does each role need to do? Are you and your group members achieving these goals?" "What task and maintenance roles did you need to take on to achieve your goal(s) and to keep the group cohesive? Did you try on a new role and take a risk?"
