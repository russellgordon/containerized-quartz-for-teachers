Everything on this site is a page, and every page lives in one of a few
folders. Once you know what each folder is *for*, you can always guess where
something lives.

| Folder | What lives there | When you go there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All Classes | One page per class: agenda and to-dos | You were away, or forget what is due |
| Concepts | The ideas — elements, voice, status… | A term comes up and you want it properly |
| Conventions | The techniques — tableau, hot seating… | Your group needs a tool for a scene |
| Tasks | The assignments, with criteria | Before, during, and after any task |
| Discussions | The conversations we return to | Preparing to talk, or thinking after |
| Setup | Routines, agreement, marks, help | Your first week, then whenever |
| Style | Pages about the site itself | You wonder why pages look this way |

## The site is a web, not a filing cabinet

The folders are only half the organisation. The other half is **links**:
every page connects to the pages it touches, so you can start anywhere and
follow the thread. A task links to the conventions it uses; a convention
links to the concepts it trains; and the **Backlinks** panel at the bottom of
any page shows every page that points back the other way.

> [!tip] Lost? Search.
> Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>) and
> type any word from any page. It is almost always faster than browsing.

The full tour of what pages can *do* — diagrams, callouts, checklists, and
the rest — is in [[What This Site Can Do]].
