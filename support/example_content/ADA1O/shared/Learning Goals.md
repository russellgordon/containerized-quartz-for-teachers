talk about drama with precision, and work inside an ensemble that people
trust. In the curriculum's own words, the three big goals are:

Put plainly, week to week that means:

- **Make** — build scenes from sources and ideas using the
  [[The Creative Process|creative process]], the
  [[The Elements of Drama|elements of drama]], and a growing kit of
  [[Conventions/index|conventions]].
- **Look and think** — watch your own and others' work
  [[Watching Like an Artist|like an artist]], and put what you notice into
  words that mean something.
- **Work like an ensemble** — rehearse, negotiate, and perform inside
  [[Our Ensemble Agreement|an agreement]] that makes risk safe.
