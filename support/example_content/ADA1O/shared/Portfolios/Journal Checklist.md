Your [[Drama Journal]] is collected at the end of each unit. Before you
hand it in, run this check — it is the same lens I read with, so anything
you catch here is something we never need to have a conversation about.
Ten minutes with this list is usually worth more than one extra entry.

## The check

**Coverage**

- [ ] There is an entry for every workshop day in the unit
- [ ] Entries were written close to the day, not reconstructed the night
      before collection — reconstructed entries always show
- [ ] At least one entry is written in role, using [[Writing in Role]]
- [ ] At least one entry responds to work I watched, using the
      describe-first habit from [[Watching Like an Artist]]

**Quality**

- [ ] Every entry names at least one specific moment — a person, a choice,
      a thing that was said (see [[What a Strong Entry Looks Like]])
- [ ] At least one entry admits a real difficulty: something that failed,
      a moment I froze, friction in the group and where it came from
- [ ] Course vocabulary appears where it belongs, used correctly — tableau,
      status, focus — not sprinkled for decoration
- [ ] My own part is named, not hidden inside "we" — what *I* suggested,
      tried, avoided, or changed

**Growth**

- [ ] Each entry ends with one concrete "what I would try next"
- [ ] At least one earlier "try next" item shows up later as a thing I
      actually tried — with a sentence about how it went

> [!tip] The last two boxes are the whole game
> Anyone can write "I will project more." The journal becomes evidence of
> growth the day a later entry says "I tried projecting to the back wall
> during the run, and for once nobody asked me to speak up" — a loop from
> intention to attempt to result. That loop is exactly what
> [[Showing Growth]] will ask you to point at, so leave yourself things to
> point at.
