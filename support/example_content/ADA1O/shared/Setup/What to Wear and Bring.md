Drama is physical. On workshop days you will be on the floor, on your feet,
and occasionally being a tree, so dress for movement.

## Wear

- Clothes you can move, stretch, and sit on the floor in.
- Flat shoes that stay on — no flip-flops, no heels. Bare feet or grip socks
  are fine on studio days when the floor is clean.
- Nothing you would be upset to crease.

> [!warning] Performance days are different
> When a task like [[Tableau Story Sequence]] is performed, your group may
> agree on simple costume pieces. Simple means simple: a hat, a scarf, a
> jacket. The task pages say when this applies.

## Bring

- [ ] Your journal (paper or device — see [[Drama Journal]] for what goes in
  it)
- [ ] Something to write with
- [ ] A water bottle — voice work is thirsty work
- [ ] Anything the current class page lists under "before our next class"

## Leave behind

Phones live in bags during workshop, not in pockets — a pocket phone ends up
on the floor within a week, and a checked phone breaks the room's focus. If
your group wants to film a rehearsal to review it, ask first; filming other
people's rough work without their okay breaks [[Our Ensemble Agreement]].
