One page per class, newest at the top of your teacher's list: the agenda we
followed and anything to do before next time. Away? Start here, then ask a
classmate what the room discovered — the page holds the plan, but the
discoveries happen live.

Our semester runs in four units:

- **Unit 1 — Building the Ensemble**: trust, the elements of drama, and the [[Tableau Story Sequence]].
- **Unit 2 — Saying Yes**: improvisation, status, and the [[Improvisation Showcase]].
- **Unit 3 — Telling Stories**: fairy tales and myths staged as our [[Scene Study from a Story]].
- **Unit 4 — Production and Performance**: production roles, simple tech, and the [[Culminating Performance]].
