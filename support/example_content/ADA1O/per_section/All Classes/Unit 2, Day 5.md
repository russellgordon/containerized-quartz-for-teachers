## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Status Walks]], numbers drawn at random
2. Paired scenes: the same three lines, played at four status gaps
3. The flip: find the moment status changes hands, and make it visible
   to the back row
4. Debrief: status is played on the body, not announced in the words
