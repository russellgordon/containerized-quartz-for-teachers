Two options on the board — "take pain relief A, a 200 mg dose that
halves every two hours, or pain relief B, a 200 mg dose that drops a
steady 30 mg every hour?" — and one rule: your preference is
worthless until mathematics backs it. Most good prompts hide a third
answer behind the two printed ones: *it depends* — and the game is
finding on what.

## How we play

1. Choose a side in silence. Commit before calculating feels safe.
2. Defend with numbers, a table, or a sketch — not with vibes.
3. Hunt the tipping point: where, exactly, does the better choice switch?

> [!example]- The pain relief duel, argued
> - "B, obviously — steady and predictable beats halving."
> - "Table it. Hour 4: A holds $200 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 50$ mg,
>   B holds $200 - 120 = 80$ mg. B is winning, as promised."
> - "Hour 8: B ran dry an hour ago — it hits zero before hour 7 —
>   but A still carries $12.5$ mg. Halving never reaches zero."
> - "So it depends on the thresholds: above what level does it
>   relieve, and below what level is the next dose safe? A lingers
>   forever; B clears completely. Different questions crown
>   different winners."
> - "And the *rate* is the deeper difference: A sheds almost 60 mg
>   in its first hour and barely 5 mg in its eighth, while B falls
>   at the same 30 mg every hour of its life. 'How fast is it
>   falling right now?' — that is the question calculus asks, and
>   [[Rates of Change]] gives it a name this course can use."

## One variation

"Would you rather ride the rollercoaster with the *taller* first
hill, or the *steeper* one?" Tall is about total drop; steep is
about drop per metre of track — an average steepness over the whole
hill, or an instantaneous one at the scariest point. Deciding which
one your stomach actually cares about is a warm-up for
[[The Rollercoaster]], where your group must defend a hill of its
own design.

> [!tip] "It depends" is a mathematical answer
> The strongest defence names the variable it depends on and the
> exact value where the choice flips. For the pain relief duel that
> variable is the threshold — and the whole duel replays with real
> stakes in [[Safe Concentration]].
