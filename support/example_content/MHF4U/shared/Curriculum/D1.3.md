sketch a graph that represents a relationship involving rate of change, as described in words, and verify with technology (e.g., motion sensor) when possible
*Sample problem:* John rides his bicycle at a constant cruising speed along a flat road. He then decelerates (i.e., decreases speed) as he climbs a hill. At the top, he accelerates (i.e., increases speed) on a flat road back to his constant cruising speed, and he then accelerates down a hill. Finally, he comes to another hill and glides to a stop as he starts to climb. Sketch a graph of John's speed versus time and a graph of his distance travelled versus time. ^text
