solve problems involving exponential and logarithmic equations algebraically, including problems arising from real-world applications
*Sample problem:* The pH or acidity of a solution is given by the equation $\text{pH} = -\log C$, where $C$ is the concentration of $[\ce{H+}]$ ions in multiples of $M = 1$ mol/L. You are given a solution of hydrochloric acid with a pH of 1.7 and asked to increase the pH of the solution by 1.4. Determine how much you must dilute the solution. Does your answer differ if you start with a pH of 2.2? ^text
