*Sample problem:* The pressure of a car tire with a slow leak is given in the following table of values:
| Time, t (min) | Pressure, P (kPa) |
|---|---|
| 0 | 400 |
| 5 | 335 |
| 10 | 295 |
| 15 | 255 |
| 20 | 225 |
| 25 | 195 |
| 30 | 170 |
Use technology to investigate linear, quadratic, and exponential models for the relationship of the tire pressure and time, and describe how well each model fits the data. Use each model to predict the pressure after 60 min. Which model gives the most realistic answer? ^text
