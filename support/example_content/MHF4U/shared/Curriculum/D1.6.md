determine, through investigation using various representations of relationships (e.g., tables of values, graphs, equations), approximate instantaneous rates of change arising from real-world applications (e.g., in the natural, physical, and social sciences) by using average rates of change and reducing the interval over which the average rate of change is determined
*Sample problem:* The distance, d metres, travelled by a falling object in t seconds is represented by d = 5t². When t = 3, the instantaneous speed of the object is 30 m/s. Compare the average speeds over different time intervals starting at t = 3 with the instantaneous speed when t = 3. Use your observations to select an interval that can be used to provide a good approximation of the instantaneous speed at t = 3. ^text
