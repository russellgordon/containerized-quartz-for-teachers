calculate and interpret average rates of change of functions (e.g., linear, quadratic, exponential, sinusoidal) arising from real-world applications (e.g., in the natural, physical, and social sciences), given various representations of the functions (e.g., tables of values, graphs, equations)
*Sample problem:* Fluorine-20 is a radioactive substance that decays over time. At time 0, the mass of a sample of the substance is 20 g. The mass decreases to 10 g after 11 s, to 5 g after 22 s, and to 2.5 g after 33 s. Compare the average rate of change over the 33-s interval with the average rate of change over consecutive 11-s intervals. ^text
