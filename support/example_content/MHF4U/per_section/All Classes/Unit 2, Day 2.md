## Agenda

1. Number talk: [[Which One Doesn't Belong]] — four rational graphs,
   one with a hole
2. Random groups: near $x = 2$, how do $y = \frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 2}$
   and $y = \frac{1}{x - 2}$ actually differ? Zoom in until you can
   say
3. Consolidate: [[Asymptotes]] — vertical, horizontal, and the hole
   that is neither
4. Conjecture at the boards: can a graph ever cross its own
   horizontal asymptote? Bring evidence, not votes
5. Verify the day's claims in [[Using Desmos]]
