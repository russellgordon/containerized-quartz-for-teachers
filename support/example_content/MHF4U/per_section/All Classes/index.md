One page per class, newest at the top of your teacher's list: the agenda we
followed and anything to do before next time. If you were away, start here —
then try the thinking task before reading anything else about it. The page
holds the plan; the discoveries happen at the boards.

Our semester runs in four units:

- **Unit 1 — Polynomial Functions**: what a graph's shape gives away —
  degree, zeros, multiplicity, symmetry — and the algebra that enforces
  it, built to order in [[The Rollercoaster]].
- **Unit 2 — Rational Functions**: division's graphs — asymptotes, holes,
  and sign arguments — put to work on a dose curve in
  [[Safe Concentration]].
- **Unit 3 — Trigonometric Functions in Radians**: a unit of angle the
  circle defines itself, exact values, and identities proved rather than
  memorised — heard out loud in [[The Sound of Two Waves]].
- **Unit 4 — Exponentials, Logarithms, and Rates of Change**: the inverse
  that needed a name, and how fast anything changes — argued in
  [[The Signature Function]], then defended before guests at
  [[The Functions Symposium]].
