## Before you run this course — what only you can decide

The safety pages in this payload are written to be honest about what
they cannot know. Ten things have to be settled against your own shop,
your own board, and your own sites before the first practical period.

1. **Which machines students may touch.** The tool pages are generic on
   purpose. Substitute your actual machines and your board's
   authorisation process.
2. **Walk-behind mowers and string trimmers.** The pages never grant
   permission for these. Your board policy and minimum-age rules decide,
   not the course.
3. **Minimum ages.** Stated in principle only — "regulations set minimum
   ages, so ask rather than assume". Insert your board's actual rules.
4. **PPE inventory.** What the school supplies, and whether
   green-triangle footwear is required or recommended for your tasks.
5. **The sites.** Shoreline access limits, woodlot trail boundaries,
   farm traffic routes and the host's own rules, and who may go where
   unaccompanied.
6. **Emergency procedure.** Where the first-aid kit is, who is
   certified, how to summon help from the woodlot if there is no signal,
   and who makes the weather cancellation call.
7. **Medical disclosure.** Allergy and auto-injector protocol, and
   heat-illness response, per your board's policy.
8. **Ticks and Lyme disease.** Check the protocol against current local
   public health guidance and your board's rules on who may remove a
   tick.
9. **Placement insurance.** The pages say "your school arranges
   coverage" — name the actual arrangement.
10. **Lockout.** The pages deliberately restrict students to unplugging
    with the plug in sight. Confirm that matches your shop's energy
    isolation procedure.

Two boundaries the pages hold on purpose, and which are worth keeping:
students are told they do not operate chainsaws, tractors, skid-steers,
ride-on mowers, or harvesting equipment, and no page names a pesticide
product or an application rate — pest control products are federally
regulated and application is licensed work.
