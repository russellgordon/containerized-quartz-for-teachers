Ask early. In a course built on real jobs, small problems get expensive
fast — a build that is out of square by a centimetre at the first corner,
a tray of seedlings that has been too wet for three days, a skill you
never quite got signed off on.

## Ways to reach me

- **In the shop, before or after class.** Every day. Best for a quick
  question, a technique you cannot get, or a tool you want to be shown
  again.
- **[[Help Sessions]]**, at the posted times, with the shop open and
  space to actually work on something with me beside you.
- **A booked conversation**, any week, for anything that needs more than
  five minutes — a plan, a mark, a placement, a problem.
- **Email**, any time, with replies on school days. Use it for anything
  you want in writing.

## What helps most

- **Bring the thing, or bring the photograph.** Thirty seconds looking at
  the actual joint beats five minutes describing it.
- **Bring your numbers.** Most build problems are a measurement, and most
  growing problems are a watering record.
- **Say what you have already tried.** "I re-cut it and it is still
  rocking on the bench" gets a real answer immediately.

> [!abstract] When a build or a crop is failing
> Come before it is dead. Nothing here is a mark for having gone well —
> a failed germination trial that you diagnosed properly is stronger
> evidence than one that worked by luck, and it is exactly what
> [[The Evidence File]] is for.
>
> Bring your records, and be ready to answer: what changed, and when did
> you first notice? For plants, start with
> [[Light, Water, Temperature, Soil]] and [[Watering Properly]]; for a
> pest or a disease, [[Pests and Diseases in Our Woodlot]]; for anything
> built, bring the drawing and the cut list.

## If you missed a period

Practical skills have to be demonstrated, not made up on paper. Tell me
which periods you missed and we will book the time — [[How Marks Work]]
explains why that matters.

## If something is physically hard

Pain, an injury, a reaction to dust or stings, an allergy, medication,
anything that affects lifting or balance, or a reason a particular task
does not work for you: tell me. It stays with me, there is almost always
another way to do the job, and it will not change how you are marked.

## Beyond this course

If you are interested in this as work — an apprenticeship, a co-op
placement, a summer job on a crew — come and say so, and we will start
early, because the good placements are arranged months ahead. See
[[The Industry Nearby]].

And if something outside class is making school hard, say so, to me or to
guidance. It works better when it starts early, and you do not have to
explain why.
