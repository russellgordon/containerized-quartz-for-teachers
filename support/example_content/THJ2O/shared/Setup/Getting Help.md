Ask early. In a course built on real jobs, small problems get expensive
fast — a build out of square at the first corner, a tray of seedlings too
wet for three days, a skill you never quite got signed off on.

## Ways to reach me

- **In the shop, before or after class.** Every day, and best for a quick
  question or a tool you want shown again.
- **[[Help Sessions]]**, at the posted times, with the shop open and
  space to work on something with me beside you.
- **A booked conversation**, any week, for anything needing more than
  five minutes — a plan, a mark, a placement, a problem.
- **Email**, any time, replies on school days, for anything in writing.

## What helps most

Bring the thing, or bring the photograph — thirty seconds looking at the
actual joint beats five minutes describing it. Bring your numbers,
because most build problems are a measurement and most growing problems
are a watering record. And say what you have already tried.

> [!abstract] When a build or a crop is failing
> Come before it is dead. A failed germination trial you diagnosed
> properly is stronger evidence than one that worked by luck, and it is
> exactly what [[The Evidence File]] is for.
>
> Bring your records, ready to say what changed and when you noticed. For
> plants, start with [[Light, Water, Temperature, Soil]] and
> [[Watering Properly]]; for a pest or disease,
> [[Pests and Diseases in Our Woodlot]]; for anything built, bring the
> drawing and the cut list.

## If you missed a period, or something is physically hard

Practical skills have to be demonstrated, not made up on paper — tell me
which periods you missed and we will book the time. [[How Marks Work]]
explains why that matters.

Pain, an injury, a reaction to dust or stings, an allergy, medication,
anything affecting lifting or balance, or a reason a particular task does
not work for you: tell me. It stays with me, there is almost always
another way to do the job, and it will not change how you are marked.

## Beyond this course

If you are interested in this as work — an apprenticeship, a co-op
placement, a summer job on a crew — say so and we will start early,
because the good placements are arranged months ahead; see
[[The Industry Nearby]]. And if something outside class is making school
hard, say so, to me or to guidance. It works better started early.
