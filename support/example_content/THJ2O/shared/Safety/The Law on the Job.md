Health and safety in Ontario is law, not a matter of how considerate an
employer happens to be. It applies to this shop and to your first job.

## The Occupational Health and Safety Act

The *Occupational Health and Safety Act* gives every worker three rights.

- **The right to know** about the hazards you will meet, with the
  information, instruction, and training to work safely. Being handed a
  machine with no training is not legal.
- **The right to participate** in health and safety at your workplace,
  through a representative or a joint committee, and to raise a concern
  without asking permission.
- **The right to refuse unsafe work.** If you believe it is likely to
  endanger you or someone else, you may refuse: report it to your
  supervisor, stay in a safe place, and it gets investigated.

The Act also says who is responsible: employers must take every
precaution reasonable in the circumstances, supervisors must enforce the
law and the required equipment, workers must work safely and report.

> [!important] On a placement, you are a worker
> A student on a work placement is a worker under the Act. The employer
> whose site you are on carries employer duties towards you, exactly as
> for the people they pay — so you are entitled to training before you
> touch equipment, and you keep the right to refuse.

## WHMIS 2015

The Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System governs hazardous
products at work, and gives you three things.

- **Labels.** A supplier label carries pictograms, a signal word
  (*Danger* or *Warning*), hazard statements saying what the product does,
  and precautionary statements saying what to do. Decant a product and
  that container gets a workplace label.
- **Safety data sheets.** Every hazardous product has a sixteen-section
  SDS available to you at work: hazards, equipment, handling, storage,
  first aid, spills. An unlabelled container is never used.
- **Training**, on WHMIS and on the products you use, before you use them.

Pest control products fall under other laws; applying them is licensed
work you will not do here. In our shop the board is the employer, your
teacher is the supervisor, you are the worker — [[Our Working Agreement]].
