## Three things worth knowing

**Links go both ways.** At the bottom of most pages is a list of pages
that link to it — the quickest way to find which class used something.

**Search is fast.** Press `Ctrl`+`K` (or `Cmd`+`K`) and type. It searches
inside pages, not just titles.

**A folded block hides something.** A line with a small arrow opens when
clicked, usually a check-yourself question.

> [!warning] Checkboxes here do not save
> The `- [ ]` lists are for reading down or copying into your own record.
> Clicking does nothing and the site does not know who you are.

## Before a practical day

The class page says a week ahead when we are in the shop, at the
woodlot, at the shoreline, or driving to the farm. Read the safety page
it links before that morning, not on the bus.
