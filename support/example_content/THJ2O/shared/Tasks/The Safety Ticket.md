**Assessed in Unit 2, and required before you use anything powered.**
Individual.

Not a quiz. A demonstration that you can work safely, checked by me, and
recorded — the way a real ticket works on a real site.

## The four parts

1. **Personal protective equipment.** Select the right protection for
   three different tasks, put it on correctly, and say what each piece
   is protecting you from and how you would know it had stopped working.
2. **Pre-start checks.** Walk through a task before starting it: the
   site, the tool, the weather, the people around you — see
   [[Before You Start Work]].
3. **Safe operation and maintenance.** For two hand tools and one
   powered tool you are authorised to use, demonstrate correct handling,
   the guard or safeguard, shutdown, and the maintenance the tool needs.
4. **Legislation, in your own words.** Explain your three rights under
   Ontario's health and safety law, what WHMIS symbols on our shop
   products mean, where the safety data sheets are, and what you would
   do about a hazard you could not fix yourself.

## What is marked

| Criterion | The standard |
| --- | --- |
| Selection | The right protection, correctly worn, without prompting |
| Judgement | You spot the hazard before it is pointed out |
| Operation | Correct, controlled, unhurried |
| Knowledge | Your rights and the WHMIS basics, accurately |
| Consistency | You work this way when nobody is assessing you |

> [!warning] This one is pass or repeat
> The safety ticket is not marked on a curve. If any part is not to
> standard, you repeat that part until it is, and you do not use the
> equipment in question until then. That is how it works on a job site
> and it is how it works here.

Everything in [[Safety/index|the safety pages]] feeds this, and
[[The Law on the Job]] is the fourth part.
