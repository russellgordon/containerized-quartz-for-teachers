Times the shop and greenhouse are open and I am in them. No appointment
needed.

| When | Where | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday, lunch | The greenhouse | Bench work, and plants that are struggling |
| Thursday, after school until 4:30 | The shop | Builds, and anything needing tools and time |
| Any morning before period 1, by arrangement | The shop | Quick questions, marks, deadlines |

During [[The Site Project]] the after-school session is on site rather
than in the shop — the class page says where that week.

> [!tip] Bring the plant, or a photograph of it
> A struggling plant tells us more in ten seconds than a description
> does in ten minutes. If you cannot carry it, photograph the whole
> plant, the leaves close up, and the growing medium.

If none of these times work, say so and we will find one. See
[[Getting Help]] for the other ways to reach me.
