Most of what happens in a newsroom evaporates as it happens —
stories get spiked, shoots get reshot, and the cut that did not make
the reel vanishes with the best evidence of your learning. Your
journal is the part that stays: the running record where every
spiked story, blurry contact sheet, and hard-won headline turns into
something you can look back through and actually *see* changing. In
this course, the journal is the portfolio — the place spiked stories
become visible growth.
