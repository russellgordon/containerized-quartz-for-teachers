## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Predict the Circuit]] — yesterday's boards with one
   component changed
2. Safety notes, then bench time: circuit variations on
   [[Breadboard a Circuit]] — brighter, dimmer, flashing
3. Measure it: multimeters out — voltage and resistance on your own
   live circuit
4. [[Electronics Calculations Practice]] beside the board, so every
   number you calculate can be checked against a real reading
5. Tools away, log it in your [[Tech Journal]]
