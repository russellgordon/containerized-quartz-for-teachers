A task is a job with a client — sometimes real, sometimes played by
the bench across the aisle. Each runs over several classes, and the
bench time is part of the job: what you do with it is assessed, as
[[How Marks Work]] explains. Every task publishes its criteria up
front, as things an observer could see at your bench, and each table
traces back to our [[Learning Goals]]. Process and safety both count.

## The five jobs

- [[The Build Sheet]] — spec a complete workstation for a real client,
  every part justified against their needs and their budget.
- [[The Network Job]] — plan, cable, build, and document a two-room
  wired network another technician could service.
- [[The Gadget]] — design and build a small device controlled by code,
  failure mode and all.
- [[The Refurb Report]] — assess one retired machine honestly and
  deliver the verdict: repair, repurpose, or recycle.
- [[The Shop Showcase]] — the culminating task: present one artifact
  to invited guests, technician-style, documentation in hand.
