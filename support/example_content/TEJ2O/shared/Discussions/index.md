Hardware looks like the neutral half of technology — no opinions,
just metal and current. But every machine in this room was mined,
manufactured, shipped, locked down, and will one day be thrown
away — and each of those steps is a decision somebody made.
Decisions have reasons, and reasons can be argued with.
