The lab provides the machines and the tools; you provide the thinking —
dressed for a workshop.

## Wear

- Closed-toe shoes on lab days, always — a dropped power supply does
  not negotiate with sandals.
- Long hair tied back and dangling jewellery off whenever tools or
  spinning fans are out. Lanyards come off at the bench.
- Nothing you would mourn if it met thermal paste.

## Bring

- [ ] Your journal or notebook (see [[Tech Journal]]) — bench notes on
  paper survive powered-off computers, which is the whole point
- [ ] Something to write with
- [ ] Your school login, memorised

## The lab provides

Screwdrivers, straps, cable and crimping gear, components, and the
machines themselves. Personal tools are welcome to stay home — matched
lab kit means every bench can follow the same steps, and nothing
walks away by accident.

> [!warning] The phone rule
> Phones live in bags during bench time — a phone above an open case is
> one fumble from an expensive lesson. The one exception: photographing
> YOUR bench's work for your journal, which is encouraged; ask before
> photographing anyone else's, per [[Our Classroom Norms]].

Forgot something? Borrow from the front desk, return it at the bell.
If anything about gear is ever a genuine barrier, tell me quietly —
see [[Getting Help]] — and we will sort it out.
