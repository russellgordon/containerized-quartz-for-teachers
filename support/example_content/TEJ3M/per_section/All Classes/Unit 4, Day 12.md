## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Which One Doesn't Belong]] — four protocols, and a defence
   that names what each one is actually for
2. Bench time: share a folder and serve a page across your bench's
   network. Then look away while the bench across the aisle breaks
   exactly one thing
3. Troubleshoot methodically, not hopefully: halve the path, test,
   halve again, and write down every test before you run it —
   [[Getting Unstuck]] works on networks too
4. Practise: [[Networking Practice]], the services block
5. Tools away, log it in your [[Tech Journal]]
