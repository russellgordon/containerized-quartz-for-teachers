## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Spot the Hazard]] — the retrospective round, using hazards
   this class actually met this semester
2. Walk the shop with a clipboard and audit our own benches against the
   agreement we all signed in week one. Findings go on the board,
   unsigned
3. Compare and name it: [[Health and Safety in the Shop]] — WHMIS, the
   Electrical Safety Code, grounding and enclosure requirements, and why
   every clause was written after somebody was hurt
4. Discussion: [[When Good Enough Is Not Safe]] — who signs, what a
   signature means, and what it costs to be wrong
5. Tools away, log it in your [[Tech Journal]]

## Things to do before our next class

- [ ] Journal: one habit of your own that a standard would not accept,
