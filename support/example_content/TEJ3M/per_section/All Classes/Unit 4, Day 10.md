## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Name That Part]] — the connector round: 8P8C, coax, fibre,
   and what each one tells you about the link behind it
2. Safety notes: crimping dies pinch hard, offcut copper flies, and any
   unknown wall port is treated as energised until proven otherwise —
   glasses on before the first cut
3. Bench time: [[Build and Test a Network]] — make the cable, test the
   cable, and only then trust the cable
4. Compare: which benches' cables passed on the first try, and what the
   failures had in common
5. Tools away, log it in your [[Tech Journal]]

## Things to do before our next class

- [ ] Journal: your cable's tester result, pair by pair, with the fault
      you fixed if it did not pass first time.
- [ ] Draw your bench's topology, labelled with addresses and masks.
