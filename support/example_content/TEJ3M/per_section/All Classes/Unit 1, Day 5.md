## Agenda

1. Warm-up debut: [[Read the Schematic]] — follow the drawing the way
   current follows wire, finger on the page
2. Bench time, the drawer challenge: pull ten unidentified parts, work
   out what each one is from what is printed on it, and confirm two of
   them with the meter
3. The manufacturer settles every argument: [[Reading a Datasheet]] —
   find the part number, then find the page that answers your question
4. Compare and name it: [[Components and Their Markings]] and
   [[Reading Schematics]] — the object in the drawer and the symbol on
   the page, matched up at last
