## Agenda

1. Warm-up debut: [[Predict the Circuit]] — a number with a unit on
   paper before the supply is switched on
2. Bench time: finish the table — one resistor, six supply voltages, the
   current recorded each time and the resistor measured cold
3. Graph current against voltage, then walk the room. Same resistor,
   same slope; different resistor, different slope. What is the slope?
4. Name it: [[Ohm's Law]] — the line you plotted, written as an equation
5. Practise: [[Ohm's Law Practice]], first block together
6. Journal: predicted and measured side by side, in the shape
   [[What a Strong Entry Looks Like]] sets out
