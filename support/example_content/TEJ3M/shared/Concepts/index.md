These pages are the settled version of what you worked out at the bench.
Read them *after* the lab, when you already have a measurement in your
notebook and a question about why it came out that way. Read them before,
and they are just words about electricity.

Grade 10 asked whether the circuit worked. This year every page here will
ask you to put a number on it first, and then hold that number up against
a meter — which is the curriculum's own expectation that you
[[A3.3|calculate the values of components using fundamental laws]],
rather than reach for the drawer and hope.
