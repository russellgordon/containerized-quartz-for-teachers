Ask early. Almost everything that becomes a serious problem in this
course was a small question in the second week.

## The ways to reach me

| How | When | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before or after class | Every day | Quick questions, and "can I check my thinking?" |
| [[Help Sessions]] | The posted times | Working on something with me in the room |
| A conversation booked with me | Any week | Anything that needs more than five minutes |
| Email | Any time; replies on school days | Something you want in writing |

## What to bring to a help session

Not "I don't get it" — that is fine as an opening, and we will get
further if you can add one of:

- The paragraph you are stuck on, and what you have tried.
- Your draft, however rough. A page of writing beats a description of
  what you intend to write.
- The question you cannot answer about your case.

## For team problems

Come as a team if you can, and bring your charter. Most team problems
turn out to be a decision nobody made — about who decides, or what
"finished" means. If a conversation with the team has not worked, come
alone and we will sort it out.

## Beyond this course

If something outside class is making this hard — health, family, work,
money — say so, to me or to guidance. I do not need the details. Ontario
schools have real supports and they work better when they start early.

> [!tip] "Can you look at this before I hand it in?"
> Yes, always, if it is more than a day before the deadline. Bring
> something written and I will tell you what would improve it most.
