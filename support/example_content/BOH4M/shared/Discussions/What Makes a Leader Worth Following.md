**The question:** which characteristics actually make people follow
someone — and are they the same ones we admire from a distance?

## Prepare

Bring one leader you would follow. Not a famous one, if you can avoid it:
a coach, a supervisor, a club president, a relative who runs something.
Come with two specific things they did.

Bring also one widely admired leader you would *not* follow, and be ready
to say why.

## The seminar

1. Round the room: your leader, and the two things they did.
2. Build the class list of characteristics on the board.
3. Test it against [[Leadership Characteristics]]: what did we name that
   the research names, and what did we leave out?
4. The argument: does integrity matter more than competence? Take a side
   and be prepared to be moved.
5. Then the one that gets least airtime — a commitment to equity and
   diversity is on the list of leadership characteristics. What does it
   look like in a decision? Give an example from your own leader if you
   can.

> [!tip] Watch for "charismatic" doing all the work
> When a description leans on presence, energy, or being inspiring, ask
> what the person actually *did*. Sometimes the answer is impressive.
> Sometimes there isn't one, and that is worth noticing before you build
> a profile around them in [[The Leadership Profile]].
