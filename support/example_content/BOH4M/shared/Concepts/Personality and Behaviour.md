People arrive at work already formed. Culture, family, schooling, and
everything that happened before Monday shape how a person reads a room,
handles pressure, and decides whether to speak up — and a manager who
treats a team as interchangeable is managing an organization chart, not
people.

## What shapes a person, and what a manager can do about it

| Influence | Shows up at work as | What management can change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cultural | What counts as polite disagreement | The norms of the room |
| Social | Confidence in a group of strangers | Who is in the room, and how it runs |
| Family | Attitudes to authority and to risk | Very little — and that is the point |
| Experience | What this person has seen go wrong before | What they are trusted with next |

The right-hand column is the useful one. You cannot manage someone's
upbringing. You can manage the conditions they work in, which is where
behaviour actually changes.

## Personality instruments, and what they can and cannot tell you

Organizations use instruments — Myers-Briggs, True Colors, measures of
emotional intelligence — for team-building and self-awareness. They are
worth knowing about, and worth being careful with.

> [!warning] Useful as a mirror, dangerous as a filter
> Used to start a conversation about how you prefer to work, an
> instrument is helpful. Used to decide who gets hired, promoted, or left
> off a project, it is doing work its evidence does not support — and in
> Ontario it can put an employer on the wrong side of the *Human Rights
> Code*. Ask any instrument the same two questions: what evidence is
> there that it measures what it claims, and what decision is about to be
> made with it?

The version worth your attention is the one about *situations*: most
people behave differently under a deadline, in an unfamiliar group, or
when they are the only person of their kind in the room. A label predicts
less than the situation does.

You will take one instrument yourself, and write about it critically, in
[[Your Management Profile]].
