Forty minutes, in the groups you will keep for
[[The Team Project]]. The point is not the tower — it is what you notice
about your group while you build it.

## The task

Your group has newspaper, one metre of tape, and twenty-five minutes to
build the tallest free-standing structure that can hold a tennis ball for
ten seconds. Nobody may speak for the first three minutes; you may plan
on paper.

## What to watch for while you work

- Who spoke first after the silent three minutes, and what happened to
  the plan on the paper?
- When did the group first disagree, and how was it settled?
- Who ended up doing what, and who decided that?
- Was there a moment when someone stopped contributing? What happened
  just before it?

## Afterwards — the part that counts

In your group, and then as a class:

1. Name the stages from [[How Groups Form]] that you can actually
   evidence in twenty-five minutes. Storming shows up fast under a
   deadline.
2. Which roles did people take? Were they the roles they take in other
   groups?
3. Which of the factors in [[What Makes a Team Work]] were present, and
   which were missing?
4. Write down one norm your group wants for the next eight weeks — one
   sentence, specific enough to hold someone to.

That norm goes straight into your team charter, which is the first
deliverable of the project.
