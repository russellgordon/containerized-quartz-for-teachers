Four questions this course argues about, each with the positions written
down so that the argument can start immediately rather than after twenty
minutes of throat-clearing.

- [[Whose Story Gets Told]] — who gets to tell a story, and what
  changes when the teller changes.
- [[Is It True, or Is It Just Fast]] — misinformation, and why the
  check is a habit rather than an instinct.
- [[Should a School Be Allowed to Read Your Posts]] — privacy, safety,
  and where the line sits.
- [[What Do We Owe a Reader]] — honesty, difficulty, and whether a
  writer may make you uncomfortable on purpose.

The rules are on [[Our Agreements]]. The short version: attack the idea,
never the person, and "you changed my mind" is the best sentence anybody
can say in this room.
