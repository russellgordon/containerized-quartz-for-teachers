**Tuesdays and Thursdays at lunch, in this room.** No appointment, no
reason needed, no shame in it — half the people who come are here because
the work is going *well* and they want it to go better.

Bring what you have, even if what you have is a blank page and a bad
mood. "I do not know how to start" is a normal thing to say to me, and it
takes about four minutes to fix.

If those two days never work for you, tell me and we will find one that
does.
