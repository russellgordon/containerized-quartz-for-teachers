By the end of this course you will be able to do four things you cannot
fully do yet.

**Read something hard and say what it means.** Not guess — read it
closely, find the evidence, and explain what the writer did to make you
think that.

**Write something a stranger would want to finish.** With a point, in
sentences that hold together, in a voice that sounds like you.

**Say what you think out loud, and listen properly when somebody else
does** — including when you disagree, and including when they change your
mind.

**Tell the difference between a source worth trusting and one that is
not**, which is the skill this year of your life will demand most often
and teach you least about.
