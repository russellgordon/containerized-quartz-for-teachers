## Which CHC2D this is

Built from the **revised course issued 25 February 2026**, effective
2026–27. The code did not change, so department resources written for
the 2018 version look correct and are not: the codes do not correspond,
and four topics are new — extreme ideologies, Nazism and the Holocaust,
the Holodomor, and Black history in Canada across two periods. Check any
inherited unit plan against the curriculum folder before reusing it.

## Two pages to review before you teach them

- **The Holodomor.** The page states the genocide designation as
  recognised by Canada, presents the scholarly consensus, and names
  Russia's contrary position as political rather than scholarly. That
  framing is defensible and it is a choice — confirm it suits your
  community before the lesson, particularly if you have Ukrainian or
  Russian-speaking families in the class.
- **Rights, Movements, and Resistance.** Ontario's fair-employment and
  fair-accommodation statutes are dated only as "the 1950s", because the
  exact years could not be verified when the page was written. If you
  want the years in front of students, check them and add them.

## Figures this course is careful about

Residential school deaths are given as the TRC's identified **3,201**
with the Commission's own statement that not all deaths were recorded,
and the NCTR's **4,141 verified** as of 2025. The pages deliberately do
not use "at least 6,000" (a spoken remark, not a finding) or "215 bodies
found" (ground-penetrating radar anomalies, not identified remains). If
a student brings those numbers in from elsewhere, that is a teachable
moment about sourcing rather than an error to correct quietly.
