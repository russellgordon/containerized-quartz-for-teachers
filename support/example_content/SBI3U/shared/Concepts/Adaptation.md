An adaptation is a heritable trait that improves survival or reproduction
in a particular environment. Three qualifications matter, and each one is
where student answers usually go wrong.

## Heritable

A tan is not an adaptation. Skin that tans is.

## In a particular environment

There is no such thing as a well-adapted organism in general. A thick coat
is an adaptation in the Arctic and a liability in the tropics, and the
environment does the deciding.

## A trade-off, always

A peacock's tail helps with mating and hurts with predators. Sickle-cell
carriers resist malaria and risk anaemia in their children. Adaptations
are compromises, and the compromise is usually the interesting part.

## Structural, behavioural, physiological

Worth sorting examples into those three: a beak shape, a migration, a
tolerance for salt. All three are heritable, all three are selected on.
