Humans are not outside evolution. We are also, unusually, a selective
force acting on nearly everything else.

## Artificial selection

Every crop and domestic animal is the result of humans doing what the
environment otherwise does: choosing which individuals reproduce. Maize
from teosinte, dogs from wolves, and the entire *Brassica* genus —
broccoli, cabbage, kale, and Brussels sprouts are one species, selected
in different directions.

The speed is the striking part. Millennia rather than millions of years,
which is direct evidence that selection can do what the theory claims.

## Selection we did not intend

- **Antibiotic resistance.** Every course of antibiotics is a selection
  event, and resistant strains are the survivors.
- **Pesticide resistance**, in the same way, in insects and weeds.
- **Fisheries.** Decades of taking the largest fish selects for
  populations that mature smaller and younger.

## The uncomfortable framing

None of these are accidents in the biological sense. They are exactly what
the theory predicts should happen when you apply strong, consistent
selection — which makes them a running experiment we did not mean to run.
