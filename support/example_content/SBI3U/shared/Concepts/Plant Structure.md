Roots, stems, leaves — and three tissue systems running through all of
them.

## The tissue systems

- **Dermal:** the outer covering, with a waxy cuticle on leaves and root
  hairs below ground.
- **Vascular:** xylem (water and minerals, upward) and phloem (sugars,
  both directions).
- **Ground:** everything else — photosynthesis, storage, support.

## The leaf, in cross-section

Cuticle, upper epidermis, palisade mesophyll (where most photosynthesis
happens, cells packed with chloroplasts), spongy mesophyll (air spaces for
gas diffusion), lower epidermis with stomata.

Every layer is a compromise between letting carbon dioxide in and keeping
water from getting out. That single trade-off explains most of the
variation in leaf shape across climates.

## Roots

Anchorage, absorption, and often storage. Root hairs multiply the
absorbing surface enormously — the same surface-area principle that runs
through the animal unit.
