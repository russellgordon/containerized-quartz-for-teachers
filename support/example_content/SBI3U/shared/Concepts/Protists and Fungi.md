## Protists

Eukaryotic, mostly single-celled, and united mainly by not being anything
else. Worth knowing in three functional groups:

- **Animal-like** (protozoa): *Amoeba*, *Paramecium*, *Plasmodium* — the
  last of which causes malaria and kills hundreds of thousands of people
  a year.
- **Plant-like** (algae): from single cells to kelp. Marine algae produce
  a large share of the oxygen you are currently breathing.
- **Fungus-like**: slime moulds and water moulds, including the organism
  that caused the Irish potato famine.

## Fungi

Not plants: no chlorophyll, no photosynthesis. They feed by secreting
enzymes and absorbing what dissolves — decomposition as a way of life.

Their body is a mesh of hyphae called a mycelium, often far larger than
the mushroom, which is only the reproductive structure. The largest known
individual organism on Earth is a fungus in Oregon.

## Why both matter here

Decomposers close every nutrient cycle. Remove them and the carbon in
dead wood stays there — which is, in fact, what happened during the
Carboniferous, and is where coal came from.
