Classification is not filing. It is a claim about relatedness, and every
time the evidence changes, the claim changes with it.

## The hierarchy

Domain, kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, species — each level
a subset of the one above. A species is written as two words, italicised,
genus capitalised: *Homo sapiens*, *Acer saccharum*.

## What it is based on

Historically, shared anatomy. Now, increasingly, shared DNA sequence —
which has reorganised whole branches. Whales sit inside the artiodactyls,
next to hippos, because the molecular evidence put them there and the
anatomical evidence agreed once someone looked at ankle bones.

## Why the names keep changing

Because taxonomy is a hypothesis about history. When a fungus turns out to
be more closely related to animals than to plants — which it is — the
classification moves. A student who finds that annoying has understood it
correctly.
