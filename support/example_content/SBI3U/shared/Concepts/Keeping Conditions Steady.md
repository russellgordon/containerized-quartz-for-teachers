Homeostasis is the maintenance of a steady internal state while the
outside changes. It is not stillness — it is constant correction.

## The parts of a control system

A **receptor** detects the change, a **control centre** compares it with a
set point, and an **effector** does something about it. Negative feedback
then switches the response off as the variable returns.

## Temperature, as the worked example

Too hot: skin vessels dilate, sweating starts, behaviour changes. Too
cold: vessels constrict, shivering starts, behaviour changes again. The
same loop, run in two directions, with the last step in both being the one
biology textbooks forget — animals move.

## Positive feedback exists too

Childbirth and blood clotting both amplify rather than damp. They are
rarer, and they always have a definite endpoint, because a runaway loop
with no endpoint is how systems fail.

Grade 12 takes this much further — see the homeostasis strand of SBI4U —
but the loop structure is the same at both levels.
