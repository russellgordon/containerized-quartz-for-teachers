Editing a person's body cells affects that person. Editing an embryo
affects everyone descended from it, none of whom can consent.

## The biology you need first

[[Genetic Technologies]] — what CRISPR does, how precisely, and where
off-target edits come from. Also the distinction between somatic and
germline, which is the whole hinge of the argument.

## The cases

- **Somatic gene therapy** for sickle-cell disease: approved, expensive,
  and working.
- **Germline editing** to prevent a fatal recessive condition: technically
  possible, banned in most countries, and done once in 2018 by a
  researcher subsequently imprisoned.
- **Enhancement**: not currently possible for anything polygenic, which
  includes almost everything people would want.

## The question

Is there a line between treating disease and enhancement? If you draw one,
where does deafness sit — and who gets to decide?
