| What | When | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A notebook and coloured pencils | Every class | Biological diagrams are labelled and drawn, not printed |
| A ruler | Every class | Scale bars on drawings; graphs |
| Closed shoes | Lab days | Dropped glass, spilled stain |
| Hair tied back | Lab days | Flames and specimens |
| A lab coat or old shirt | Dissection and staining days | Stains do not wash out |

Coloured pencils are not decoration. A biological drawing uses colour to
distinguish tissue types, and a pencil drawing you can erase is worth more
than a pen drawing you cannot.

> [!tip] The microscope drawing rule
> Draw what you SEE, not what the textbook says you should see. If your
> onion cells look squashed, draw them squashed and say why in the note
> beneath.
