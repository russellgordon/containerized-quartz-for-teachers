> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Format:** three pages. **Individually.** **Due:** end of Unit 2.
> **Assessed on:** Knowledge, Thinking, Communication.

Take one case where evolution is observable within human timescales, and
lay out the evidence the way a scientist would.

## Choose one

Antibiotic resistance in *Staphylococcus* · pesticide resistance in
Colorado potato beetles · industrial melanism in peppered moths ·
Darwin's finches after the 1977 drought · lactase persistence in adult
humans · herbicide-resistant weeds in Ontario fields.

## What to produce

1. **The population before**, with its variation described.
2. **The selective pressure**, named precisely and dated.
3. **The change**, with actual data — frequencies, not adjectives.
4. **The mechanism**, connecting your case to
   [[Natural Selection]] step by step.
5. **What would falsify it.** State what evidence would have shown the
   change was NOT selection — genetic drift, migration, or a measurement
   artefact.

Point 5 is the difference between a report and an argument.
