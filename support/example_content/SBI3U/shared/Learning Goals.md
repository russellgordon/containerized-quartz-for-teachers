By the end of this course you will be able to look at a living thing and
ask productive questions about it: what is it related to, how does it
work, and how did it come to be that way.

In plain words, you will be able to:

- classify an organism and explain what the classification is based on;
- explain how evolution works, and what evidence supports it;
- predict the outcome of a genetic cross, and read a pedigree;
- explain how an animal's systems keep it alive, and how they interact;
- explain how a plant makes food, moves water, and reproduces.
