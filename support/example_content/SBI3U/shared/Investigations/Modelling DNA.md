> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Time:** one class. **Pairs.** The model is photographed, not kept.

Build a section of DNA from paper or kit components, then use it to work
out replication and transcription for yourself.

## Method

1. Build a double-stranded section at least eight base pairs long, with
   the backbones antiparallel and the pairing correct.
2. Separate the strands and build the complementary strand for each. You
   now have two identical molecules — semi-conservative replication, done
   with your hands.
3. Transcribe one strand to mRNA, remembering uracil.
4. Translate your mRNA using a codon chart.

## Analysis

1. Change ONE base in the middle of your sequence. Re-translate. What
   happened to the protein?
2. Now DELETE one base instead and re-translate. Compare the damage.
   Which mutation type is worse, and why?
3. Why must the strands be antiparallel? Answer from your model, not from
   memory.
