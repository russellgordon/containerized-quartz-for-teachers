> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Time:** one class. **Pairs.** Labelled drawings due next class.

Pond water, prepared slides, and the first serious use of the microscope
this year.

## Safety

Pond water is not sterile. Gloves are not needed but hands are washed
afterwards; nothing is tasted; slides go in the sharps bin.

## Method

1. Prepare a wet mount of pond water. Start at low power, find something,
   THEN increase magnification.
2. Observe prepared slides of bacteria, *Paramecium*, *Amoeba*, and a
   filamentous alga.
3. Draw four organisms, each with: a title, a magnification, a scale bar,
   and labels for what you can actually see.

## The drawing rules

Pencil, single clear lines, no shading, labels with straight lines that do
not cross. Draw what is there — if the *Amoeba* was moving and you saw
three shapes, say so in a note.

## Analysis

1. Sort your four organisms into kingdoms and justify each with an
   observation you made, not one you looked up.
2. Estimate the size of one organism using the field of view diameter.
3. Which of your organisms could you NOT have classified without seeing
   it move? What does that say about classification from preserved
   specimens?
