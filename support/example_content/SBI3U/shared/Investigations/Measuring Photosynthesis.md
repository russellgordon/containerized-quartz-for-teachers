> [!abstract] At a glance
> **Time:** one class. **Groups of three.** A graph with a limiting
> factor identified.

Leaf discs in sodium bicarbonate solution sink; as they photosynthesise,
oxygen collects and they rise. Time to fifty per cent floating is your
rate.

## Safety

Bicarbonate solution and syringes only. The lamp gets hot; keep it clear
of the beaker and off the bench edge.

## Method

1. Cut ten discs from a leaf with a hole punch. Draw the air out under
   the syringe so they sink.
2. Place them in bicarbonate solution at a set distance from the lamp.
3. Record the time for five of the ten to float.
4. Repeat at four distances — and remember light intensity falls with the
   SQUARE of distance, which your graph should account for.

## Analysis

1. Plot rate against light intensity — [[Graphing Data]] has the
   conventions. Where does the curve flatten?
2. At the flat part, light is no longer limiting. Name two candidates for
   what is, and design a test that would distinguish them.
3. Why bicarbonate rather than plain water? Answer with the equation from
   [[Photosynthesis]].
