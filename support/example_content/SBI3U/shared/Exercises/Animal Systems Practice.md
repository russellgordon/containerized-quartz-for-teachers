**1.** Trace a molecule of oxygen from the air to a muscle cell, naming
every structure it passes.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> Nose or mouth → trachea → bronchus → bronchiole → alveolus → across two
> cell layers → capillary → binds haemoglobin → pulmonary vein → left
> atrium → left ventricle → aorta → artery → arteriole → capillary →
> diffuses into the muscle cell.

**2.** Why is the left ventricle wall thicker than the right?

> [!success]- Answer 2
> It pumps to the whole body against much higher resistance; the right
> pumps only to the lungs.

**3.** Give two features of the small intestine that increase surface
area, and say why that matters.

> [!success]- Answer 3
> Folds and villi (and microvilli on the villi). More surface means more
> absorption per unit length, which is the constraint every exchange
> surface faces.

**4.** Breathing rate rises during exercise. What is actually detected?

> [!success]- Answer 4
> Rising carbon dioxide (and falling pH), not falling oxygen — see
> [[The Respiratory System]].
