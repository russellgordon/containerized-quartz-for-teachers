**1.** Put these in order from broadest to narrowest: family, domain,
genus, order, kingdom, species, class, phylum.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> Domain, kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, species.

**2.** Why is *Homo sapiens* written that way, and what is wrong with
"homo Sapiens"?

> [!success]- Answer 2
> Genus capitalised, species lower case, both italicised (or underlined
> when handwritten). The convention is international, so a name means the
> same thing in every language.

**3.** A newly found organism is single-celled, has no nucleus, and lives
in a hot spring at 90 °C. Which domain, and what else would you check?

> [!success]- Answer 3
> Probably Archaea. Check membrane lipid chemistry and ribosomal RNA
> sequence — the habitat alone is suggestive, not decisive, since some
> bacteria are also thermophiles.

**4.** Why is Protista considered an unsatisfactory kingdom?

> [!success]- Answer 4
> Because it groups organisms that are not each other's closest
> relatives — it is defined by what its members are not. See
> [[The Kingdoms of Life]].
