**1.** Write the equation for photosynthesis and say where the oxygen
comes from.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> $\ce{6CO2 + 6H2O ->[light] C6H12O6 + 6O2}$. The oxygen comes from
> splitting water, not from carbon dioxide — shown with isotope labelling.

**2.** Explain how water reaches the top of a 30 m tree without a pump.

> [!success]- Answer 2
> Transpiration pull: evaporation from the leaves pulls a cohesive column
> of water up the xylem, helped by adhesion to the walls.

**3.** A plant closes its stomata at midday on a hot dry day. What does it
gain and what does it lose?

> [!success]- Answer 3
> Gains: reduced water loss. Loses: carbon dioxide entry, so
> photosynthesis slows. The central trade-off of
> [[Transport in Plants]].

**4.** Why does a field of genetically identical crop plants carry a risk
that a varied field does not?

> [!success]- Answer 4
> No variation means no individuals resistant to a new pathogen — the
> whole crop is susceptible at once. See [[Plant Reproduction]].
