**1.** A student says giraffes have long necks because they stretched to
reach leaves. What is wrong, and what is the correct account?

> [!success]- Answer 1
> Acquired characteristics are not inherited. The correct account:
> variation in neck length existed; longer-necked individuals survived and
> reproduced better; the allele frequencies shifted over generations.

**2.** Bacteria in a patient become resistant during a course of
antibiotics. Did the antibiotic cause the resistance mutations?

> [!success]- Answer 2
> No. The mutations were already present in a few cells; the antibiotic
> killed the rest, leaving the resistant ones to multiply. Selection, not
> instruction.

**3.** Distinguish homologous from analogous structures, with an example
of each.

> [!success]- Answer 3
> Homologous: same underlying structure, different function — a bat's wing
> and a whale's flipper. Analogous: same function, different structure — a
> bat's wing and an insect's wing.

**4.** Two populations of the same species are separated by a new
reservoir. What has to happen before they are separate species?

> [!success]- Answer 4
> Enough genetic divergence — through different selection, mutation, and
> drift — that they can no longer produce fertile offspring together. See
> [[Speciation]].
