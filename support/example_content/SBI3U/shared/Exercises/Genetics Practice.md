Use $T$ for tall (dominant) and $t$ for short.

**1.** Cross $Tt \times Tt$. Give genotype and phenotype ratios.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> Genotypes 1 $TT$ : 2 $Tt$ : 1 $tt$. Phenotypes 3 tall : 1 short.

**2.** A tall plant is crossed with a short one and half the offspring are
short. What was the tall parent's genotype?

> [!success]- Answer 2
> $Tt$. A $TT$ parent could produce no short offspring at all.

**3.** In snapdragons, red × white gives all pink. What is this called,
and what does pink × pink give?

> [!success]- Answer 3
> Incomplete dominance. Pink × pink gives 1 red : 2 pink : 1 white.

**4.** A colour-blind man and a woman who is a carrier have children. What
fraction of their sons and daughters are expected to be colour blind?

> [!success]- Answer 4
> Half the sons, and half the daughters. Sex-linked recessive, with an
> affected father — see [[Beyond Mendel]].

**5.** Why does a 3:1 ratio not mean exactly three of every four
offspring?

> [!success]- Answer 5
> Because each fertilisation is independent. The ratio is what large
> numbers approach, which is why [[A Monohybrid Cross]] counts hundreds.
