Your first [[Biology Journal]] entry is written in class, so the format is
never a mystery afterwards.

## The prompt

After [[Microscopy of Microorganisms]], write three quarters of a page:

1. **In your own words:** what makes a cell eukaryotic? Do not use the
   word "nucleus" more than once.
2. **A drawing:** one organism you actually saw, with a title,
   magnification, and labels — the conventions are on
   [[Biological Drawing]].
3. **Still murky:** one question you would ask if there were no cost to
   asking it.

## What a good third question looks like

- Weak: "I do not understand protists."
- Better: "How do we know *Amoeba* is not just a very large bacterium?"
- Best: "If protists are not a real group, why is it still in the
  textbook?"

The last one is a question about how science handles categories that
stopped working, and it has a genuinely interesting answer.
