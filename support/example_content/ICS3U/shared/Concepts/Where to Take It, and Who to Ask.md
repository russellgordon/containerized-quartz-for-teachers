When a machine reaches the end of its life with you, it does not have
to reach the end of its life. Ontario has a settled answer for where it
goes, and there are organisations whose whole job is to help. This page
is the short list, so that "I did not know where to take it" stops
being a reason.

## Recycling and reuse

- **Recycle My Electronics / EPRA Ontario** — the province's approved
  electronics recycling program, funded by the companies that put the
  products on the market. Drop-off is free to residents at approved
  depots across Ontario, and their site maps the nearest one.
- **Your municipality** — most run a household hazardous- and
  electronic-waste depot, or a collection day. Municipal waste
  departments will tell you what they take and when.
- **Renewed Computer Technology**, which delivers the **Computers for
  Schools Plus** program in Ontario — refurbishes donated computers for
  schools, libraries, non-profits, and low-income households, and runs
  paid youth internships doing the refurbishing. A working machine
  given here has years left in it.
- **Retailers and manufacturers** — many take back old equipment when
  you buy new, which is the easiest moment to actually do it.

## Guidance and rules

- **Resource Productivity and Recovery Authority (RPRA)** — the
  provincial authority that oversees Ontario's recycling programs and
  holds producers to them.
- **Ontario Ministry of the Environment, Conservation and Parks** —
  provincial environmental policy and regulation, including waste.
- **Environment and Climate Change Canada** — federal environmental
  data, reporting, and guidance.

> [!tip] Before it leaves your hands
> Wipe it. A donated laptop with your files still on it is a privacy
> incident wearing a good deed's clothes. Sign out of every account,
> then erase the drive properly — the operating system's own "erase and
> reinstall" is the reliable version, not dragging folders to the bin.
> [[Keeping Data Safe]] explains why deleted is not gone.

Details change: programs are renamed, depots move, rules are rewritten.
Check the current site before you act on any of this, and treat a page
that has not been updated in years with suspicion — including this one.
