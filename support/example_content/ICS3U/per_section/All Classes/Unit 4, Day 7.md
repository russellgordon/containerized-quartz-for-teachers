## Agenda

1. Set up: chairs, cables, and a working machine per pair, before a
   single guest arrives

   by you, and you keep your hands off the keyboard
3. The handover itself: the files, the written instructions, and the
   promise about what happens next
4. Structured peer critique: one thing that worked, one honest question
5. Hand in [[Final Reflection]] and your [[Code Journal]]
