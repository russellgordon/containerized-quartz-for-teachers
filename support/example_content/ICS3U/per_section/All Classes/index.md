One page per class, newest at the top of your teacher's list: the agenda
we followed and anything to do before next time. Away? Start here, run
the warm-up yourself, and attempt the build before reading anything
about it — the page holds the plan, but the ideas earn their names in
the room.

Our semester runs in four units:

- **Unit 1 — Programs That Decide**: instructions precise enough for a
  machine, variables, input and output, and the decisions a program
  makes, ending in [[The Helper Script]].
- **Unit 2 — Repetition and Data**: loops discovered the hard way,
  lists, text, and tables, ending in [[The Data Digest]].
- **Unit 3 — Structure, Files, and Design**: functions, decomposition,
  and data that survives the program closing, ending in [[The Toolbox]].
- **Unit 4 — Building for Someone Else**: interfaces with dignity,
  testing with real people, debugging as a craft, and what computing
  costs the world — ending at [[Launch Day]].

Running underneath Units 3 and 4 is [[The Community App]]: a real person
in the school or the neighbourhood, a modest problem in their own words,
and an app you build *with* them and hand over so they can keep using it
long after you have gone.
