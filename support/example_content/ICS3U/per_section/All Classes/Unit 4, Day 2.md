## Agenda

1. Warm-up: [[Spot the Bug]] — a function that returns the wrong thing,
   very quietly
2. Build period: the core feature first, the clever extras never
3. Stuck? Work the list in [[Getting Unstuck]] before you put a hand up
4. Design review in threes at the end: show your screens, name the one
   thing you cut, and say what it bought you
5. Log it in your [[Code Journal]]

## Things to do before our next class

- [ ] Milestone: your core feature runs end to end. Badly is fine.
- [ ] Arrange the time and place your client will test it with you.
