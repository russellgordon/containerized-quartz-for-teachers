## Agenda

1. Set up: machines, chairs, and the project history open on screen,
   before a single guest walks in
2. Partners arrive — [[The Handover]]: your partner runs the software
   in front of the room, and you keep your hands off the keyboard
3. The package changes hands: the code, the documentation, the known
   limits, and the name of who to ask next
4. Structured team review: what the plan said, what actually happened,
   and one recommendation for whoever inherits this
5. Hand in [[Final Reflection]] and your [[Code Journal]]
