## Extra help

| When | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Monday, lunch | The computer lab |
| Wednesday, after school until 4:15 | The computer lab |

Drop in — no appointment, no need to tell me first. Bring the specific
thing you are stuck on: one error message beats "my code doesn't work".
Team time counts too: bring a merge conflict, a design disagreement,
or a piece of inherited code nobody on your team can decipher.

If your program runs but does the wrong thing, bring the input you gave
it and what you expected instead. That pair is usually the whole
diagnosis.
