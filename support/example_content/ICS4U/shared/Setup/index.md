Everything you need to know before the building starts. If you were
here for Grade 11 the page names will look familiar, and every one of
them has changed underneath — because this year you work in a team,
on code you did not write, for somebody who keeps it afterwards. Read
these in your first week, and come back whenever a routine feels
fuzzy.

- [[How This Class Works]] — the workshop rhythm, and the two things
  that are new: version control from day one, and other people's code
  treated as a skill rather than a punishment.
- [[Our Classroom Norms]] — broken programs welcome, honesty about
  help, reviews about the code and never the person. The most
  important page here.
- [[What to Bring]] — a notebook, your login, and the phone rule.
- [[How Marks Work]] — where the evidence comes from, why the team
  project is the biggest single piece, and how your individual
  contribution inside it is actually seen.
- [[Getting Help]] — the order to try things in, including the two
  kinds of stuck that only exist on a team.
