The lab provides the machines; you provide the thinking. The list is
short, and it is nearly the same as last year — with one addition
that matters more than it looks.

## Every class

- [ ] Your journal or a notebook — yes, paper. Sketching a class
      diagram, a call stack, or a plan away from the keyboard is a
      professional habit, not a school one, and [[Code Journal]]
      explains what goes in it
- [ ] Something to write with
- [ ] Your school login details, memorised. Class time spent on
      password resets is class time gone
- [ ] The name you commit under, set up and consistent. Your commits
      are the record of what you contributed to the team project, and
      commits made under a different name on a different machine do
      not count for you — see [[How Marks Work]]

## Sometimes, and I will say when

- Headphones or earbuds for days with video or audio work — one ear
  only when a teammate is talking.
- Your own device is welcome once you have Python and version control
  working on it, but it is never required. Everything in this course
  runs on the lab machines and on free software. If you do use your
  own, make sure your work reaches the shared repository the same day;
  code that exists only on your laptop is code your team does not
  have.
- For partner meetings: your questions written out, and a way to take
  notes that does not put a screen between you and the person
  talking.

> [!warning] The phone rule
> Phones live in bags during build time. The device in front of you is
> already a computer; the one in your pocket is a distraction machine
> wearing the same costume. Filming or photographing anyone's screen
> or work without their okay breaks [[Our Classroom Norms]], and that
> absolutely includes anything belonging to a community partner.

Forgot something? Borrow from the front desk, return it at the bell,
and nobody will make it a thing. If equipment or connectivity at home
is ever a genuine barrier — and in a course with a team project it
can be — tell me quietly, see [[Getting Help]], and we will sort it
out. That includes the case where your team is meeting outside class
and you cannot get there.
