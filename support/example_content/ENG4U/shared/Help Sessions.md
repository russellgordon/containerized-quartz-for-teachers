**Mondays and Thursdays at lunch, in this room.**

Bring the text, the draft, or the question. "I have read the chapter
three times and I have nothing" is the most common opening line here and
it is a completely reasonable one — the fix is usually twenty minutes and
a pencil.

In Grade 12 the most common thing students need is not an explanation.
It is somebody to read the paragraph they cannot see straight any more.
That takes ten minutes and it is the whole reason these sessions exist.
