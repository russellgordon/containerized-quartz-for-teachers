Unseen passages, answered under time. This is the examination's largest
single section and the one that improves fastest with practice.

**1.** Read this and answer in one sentence each: what has happened, and
how do you know?

> He counted the tins twice. Then he moved four of them to the back of
> the cupboard, where the light did not reach, and closed the door
> quietly, the way you close a door when somebody is asleep.

> [!success]- Answer 1
> What: he is hiding food. How: the second count, the deliberate move out
> of sight, and the simile about somebody sleeping — which tells you
> there is another person in the house he does not want to wake or to
> know.

**2.** From the same passage: what does the last clause tell you about
the relationship?

> [!success]- Answer 2
> That care and concealment are happening at once. He is quiet *for*
> somebody — the same person he is hiding the tins from. A weaker answer
> says "he is sneaky"; the passage will not support only that.

**3.** Identify the technique and its effect: *the light did not reach*.

> [!success]- Answer 3
> Understatement doing the work of a metaphor: the cupboard's dark is
> literal, and it also names what he is doing to the household — keeping
> something out of the light. Naming the device is worth nothing without
> the second half of that sentence.

**4.** Under time: read an unseen paragraph for four minutes and write
one analytical paragraph in eight. Then check it against
[[The Extended Essay]].

> [!success]- Answer 4
> No fixed answer. The check: claim in sentence one, evidence quoted by
> sentence three, and at least two sentences of analysis. If the
> paragraph ends where the quotation ends, it is not finished.

**5.** The examination asks "how does the writer create X?" Rewrite that
question in your own words before answering it.

> [!success]- Answer 5
> "What did the writer do, in the words on this page, that produced X in
> me?" That rewrite is the whole method: it forces you back to the text
> rather than to your impression of it.
