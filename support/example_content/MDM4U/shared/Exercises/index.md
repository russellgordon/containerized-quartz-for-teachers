Every set below lands *after* the thinking — after the exploration,
the boards, and the concept page that consolidated them. This is the
data management room, so the answers here do two things at once. They
show the arithmetic in full, and they say what the number **means**,
because a probability you cannot interpret is a probability you cannot
use. Where a question asks for a judgement rather than a value — is
this correlation causal, is this sample defensible, would you report
the mean or the median — the answer explains the reasoning and admits
where reasonable people might differ. Nothing here is timed, counted,
or collected. Ever.

A note on tools. Anything in the first four sets is meant to be done
by hand, because the counting is the learning. From
[[Distributions Practice]] onward, use technology for the arithmetic
and spend your attention on setup and interpretation —
[[Using a Spreadsheet for Statistics]] and [[Using Desmos]] both do
the tedious parts, and neither will tell you which model applies.

Stuck partway through a set? That is the set working. Start with
[[Getting Unstuck]], and remember that [[Checking Your Own Work]]
beats checking ours.
