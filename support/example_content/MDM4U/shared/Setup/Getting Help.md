Stuck is a normal working condition in mathematics — being stuck WELL
is a skill this course deliberately teaches (see [[Getting Unstuck]]).
But there is a difference between productive struggle and spinning,
so here is the order to try things in:

1. **Read the problem again, out loud if you can.** A surprising
   fraction of stuck is really "answered a different question" —
   especially here, where "at least one" and "exactly one" sit one
   word apart.
2. **Check the site.** The class page says what we did; the
   [[Concepts/index|concept pages]] explain every idea we use; every
   [[Exercises/index|practice set]] has worked answers folded below
   it.
3. **Ask your table.** Explaining where exactly you got lost is half
   the diagnosis — and remember the norm: helpers ask questions, they
   don't hand over answers.
4. **Ask me in class.** Work time is what it is for. Problems raised
   during work time are usually fixed in minutes.
5. **Come to a help session.** Times are on [[Help Sessions]]. Bring
   the specific thing — "the whole probability unit" is hard to help
   with; one half-finished tree diagram is easy.

> [!tip] Name where it broke
> "I can't do probability" is hard to help. "I can compute the chance
> that the first card is an ace, but I lose track of what the second
> probability should be once the first card is gone" — that one is
> fixed in five minutes. The habit of locating the exact broken step
> is [[Showing Your Thinking]] pointed at yourself.

A special case worth naming: if the trouble is with
[[The Culminating Investigation]] and it is not mathematical — you
cannot find data, or the data you found will not answer your question
— come early rather than late. That kind of stuck does not resolve on
its own, and it is much cheaper to change your question in the third
unit than in the last week. [[Choosing a Data Set]] and
[[Where to Find Real Data]] solve most of it before we even talk.

One more thing: if the trouble is bigger than mathematics — time,
workload, something at home — tell me straight away, in whatever way
is easiest. Marks can wait; see [[Our Classroom Norms]] for the room
this class tries to be.
