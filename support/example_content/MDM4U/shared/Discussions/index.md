Some days the markers go down and the talking *is* the mathematics. A
claim only becomes knowledge after other people have pushed on it —
with questions, counter-examples, and better claims — and the pushing
is always aimed at ideas, never at the classmate brave enough to
voice one. In a course where every number has people behind it and
every conclusion comes with a margin of error, that habit is not a
nicety; it is the method. Argue hard with the claim; thank the person
who made it.

Four conversations we return to all semester:

- [[When Will I Use This]] — the oldest question, and the course with
  the least trouble answering it
- [[Mistakes Are Data]] — the museum of reasonable wrong turns; never
  erase
- [[Why Struggle Is the Point]] — stuck-and-thinking versus spinning
- [[What Makes a Model Good]] — accuracy is not the same as fairness,
  and neither is the same as truth

Come with opinions. Expect to leave with different ones — and with a
clearer sense of which of your opinions were ever evidence.
