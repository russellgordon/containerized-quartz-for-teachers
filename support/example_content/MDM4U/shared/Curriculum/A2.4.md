*Sample problem:* A school is 5 blocks west and 3 blocks south of a student’s home. Determine, in a variety of ways (e.g., by drawing the routes, by using Pascal’s triangle, by using combinations), how many different routes the student can take from home to the school by going west or south at each corner. ^text
