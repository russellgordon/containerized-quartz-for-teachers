determine the theoretical probability, $P_i$ (i.e., a value from 0 to 1), of each outcome of a discrete sample space (e.g., in situations in which all outcomes are equally likely), recognize that the sum of the probabilities of the outcomes is 1 (i.e., for $n$ outcomes, $P_1 + P_2 + P_3 + \ldots + P_n = 1$), recognize that the probabilities $P_i$ form the probability distribution associated with the sample space, and solve related problems
*Sample problem:* An experiment involves rolling two number cubes and determining the sum. Calculate the theoretical probability of each outcome, and verify that the sum of the probabilities is 1. ^text
