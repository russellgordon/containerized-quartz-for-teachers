Everything for this course lives on this site, and it rewards being
used in a particular way. Five minutes here saves you an hour of
wandering mid-semester.

## Start where the site starts

The landing page for your section always shows the **most recent
class** — the warm-up, the thinking task, what is due. If you were
away, that page is your first stop; if you were here, it is still the
fastest answer to "was that the one where we sample *without*
replacement again?" Older class pages sit right behind it, one per
day, so a week away is five short pages read in order.

## Three habits that make the site fast

1. **Search first.** Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or
   <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>) and type any word — "hypergeometric",
   "margin of error", a task name, a phrase you half remember. Search
   is almost always faster than browsing folders.
2. **Hover before you click.** Rest your pointer on any link — try
   [[Conditional Probability]] — and the page appears in a small
   preview window. You can check a definition mid-problem without
   losing your place.
3. **Read the backlinks.** At the bottom of every page is a list of
   every page that links *to* it. On [[The Normal Distribution]], the
   backlinks show each class and task where that idea earned its keep
   — a ready-made review list nobody had to build.

## Try before you peek

Exercise pages such as [[Conditional Probability Practice]] fold
their answers away behind a click. That fold is load-bearing: an
honest attempt before peeking teaches more than ten confirmations
after. Commit to an answer, defend it to yourself, *then* check — the
order is the whole trick.

The short version of the map: class pages record what happened,
Concepts pages explain ideas, Exercises build fluency, Data Sets is
where you go hunting for something real to analyse, and your
[[Math Journal]] instructions live in Portfolios. The full map is
[[How This Site Is Organised]]; the tour of what pages can do —
previews, rendered mathematics, folded answers — is
[[What This Site Can Do]].
