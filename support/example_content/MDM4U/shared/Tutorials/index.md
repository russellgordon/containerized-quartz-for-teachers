Tutorials are how-to pages: not ideas to understand but skills to use.
Nobody reads them straight through — you read the one you need in the
moment you need it, and every one of these moments arrives for every
student in this course, usually more than once.

- [[Using This Site]] — five minutes that make the whole site fast
- [[Showing Your Thinking]] — reasoning made visible, on paper and at
  the boards
- [[Using a Spreadsheet for Statistics]] — the functions that do the
  arithmetic, and the checks that keep it honest
- [[Using Desmos]] — a cloud of points, a line, and its residuals
- [[Simulating with Python]] — answering a probability question by
  doing the thing a hundred thousand times
- [[How to Study for Math]] — retrieval beats re-reading, every time
- [[Getting Unstuck]] — how to be stuck well, on purpose
- [[Checking Your Own Work]] — the habit that makes marks boring

> [!tip] When to come back
> Stuck on a counting problem at the boards? [[Getting Unstuck]].
> Distributions quiz next week? [[How to Study for Math]]. Just
> downloaded 4,000 rows and not sure where to start?
> [[Using a Spreadsheet for Statistics]]. Just computed a probability
> you find surprising? [[Simulating with Python]] will tell you
> within a minute whether to trust it. These pages are built to be
> reread.
