## Agenda

1. Number talk: [[True or False]] — "taking a card out of the deck
   changes the odds for every card still in it"
2. Random groups: five cards from a standard deck. Find the chance of
   exactly two hearts — first pretending each card goes back, then
   honestly. Predict the gap before you compute it
3. Discuss: two answers, close but not equal. Which real situation
   does each one actually describe?
4. Name it: [[The Hypergeometric Distribution]] — sampling without
   replacement, where the trials talk to each other
5. Practise: [[Distributions Practice]], third set
