## Agenda

1. Number talk: [[Number Strings]] — predict the next value, then one
   far outside the string, then say which prediction you trust
2. Random groups: fit a line to yesterday's scatter by eye, then by
   technology. Whose leftovers are smaller, and where is each line at
   its worst?
3. Name it: regression and residuals — and the difference between a
   line that fits and a line that predicts
4. Discuss: [[What Makes a Model Good]] — accuracy is one answer, and
   it is not the only one
5. Investigation checkpoint: data in hand, and [[Citing Your Sources]]
   before a single number enters your report
6. Practise: [[Regression and Inference Practice]], first set
