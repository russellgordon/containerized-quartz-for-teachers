## Agenda

1. Number talk: [[Visual Patterns]] — a growing arrangement of tiles:
   how many at step $10$, and how do you know without drawing it?
2. Random groups: five people, five chairs — count the seatings. Then
   eight people and five chairs. Then five chairs again, with two
   people who refuse to sit beside each other
3. Discuss: every board wrote a falling product. Where did the falling
   come from, and what does each factor stand for?
4. Name it: [[Permutations]] — counting when order carries meaning
5. Practise: [[Permutations and Combinations Practice]], first set
