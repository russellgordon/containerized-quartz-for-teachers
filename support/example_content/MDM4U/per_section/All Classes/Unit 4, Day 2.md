## Agenda

1. Number talk: [[Estimation Duels]] — how many people must a pollster
   ask before the result is worth printing?
2. Random groups: simulate two hundred polls of fifty people, drawn
   from a population whose truth you already know. How often does a
   poll miss, and by how much?
3. Name it: margin of error and confidence level — what "accurate to
   within three percentage points, nineteen times out of twenty"
   promises, and what it very carefully does not
4. Tool: [[Simulating with Python]] again — one loop, two hundred
   polls, one honest histogram
5. Practise: [[Regression and Inference Practice]], third set
