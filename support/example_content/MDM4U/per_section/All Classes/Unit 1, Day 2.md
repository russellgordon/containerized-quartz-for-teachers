## Agenda

1. Number talk: [[Would You Rather]] — guess a four-letter, three-digit
   licence plate, or guess a seven-digit phone number? Defend your pick
2. Random groups: [[Counting Without Counting]] — arrangements far too
   numerous to list, counted anyway
3. Discuss: every board ended up multiplying, and nobody was told to.
   Compare where each group's multiplication came from
4. Name it: [[The Fundamental Counting Principle]] — the rule your
   boards used before it had a name
5. Practise: [[Counting Practice]], first set — with
   [[Showing Your Thinking]] on what "show your work" means here
