## The rhythm of a class

Most classes in this course follow the same shape, so you always know what
is coming:

1. Look together: one work, three minutes, no talking
2. Demonstration of today's technique — [[Techniques/index|Techniques]]
3. Studio time — [[Studio Time/index|Studio Time]]
4. Clean-up, with ten minutes to spare
5. Quick critique: two works, two comments each — [[Critiques/index|Critiques]]

## What to do when you are stuck

Ask in class, ask at [[Help Sessions]], or check [[Getting Help]]. Being
stuck is ordinary. Staying stuck quietly is the only mistake.
