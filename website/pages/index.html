What it is

A plantoir is a dibber — the simple hand tool that opens a hole at the right depth so a seedling can be set in and take root. This app does the same for teaching materials.

Write in Obsidian, preview locally, publish when you're ready. No content management system, no fifteen clicks to post a handout, and no platform holding your work hostage when you move schools.

How it works

  1. Add a course.
  2. Write your notes as Markdown files, in Obsidian or any text editor.
  3. Preview the site on your own machine, as often as you like.
  4. Publish when you're ready. Your students get a real website.

See what it can do, or read how it fits into an ordinary teaching week.

What students see

Quick, searchable, and readable on a phone at the back of the room.

Mathematics is typeset properly. Diagrams are drawn from your notes rather than pasted in as pictures. Every page can link to every other.
